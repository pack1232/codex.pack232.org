*Original message from Glenmont PTA (Farrin Wagoner):*

Dear Community Partners,

We're excited to invite all Bethlehem clubs, businesses, non-profits, community groups, and organizations to participate in an upcoming **Bethlehem Activities Fair!**

**TO REGISTER:** https://forms.gle/pq1J8FPN7KtGVZUw5

| | |
|---|---|
| **Event** | Activities Fair for K-6th Grade Students |
| **Date** | Wednesday, March 26, 2025 |
| **Time** | 6:30 PM - 8:00 PM (Set-up begins at 6 PM, doors open at 6:30 PM) |
| **Location** | Glenmont Elementary School, 328 Route 9W, Glenmont, NY 12077 |
| **Cost** | $20 per booth for businesses; Free for non-profit organizations |
| **Registration Deadline** | March 14, 2025 |

**Why Participate?**
This is a wonderful opportunity to connect with local families, network with other vendors and gain valuable exposure for your organization. The event is open to all families in Bethlehem, ensuring a wide audience.

**Booth Details**
Each booth will include one standard-size folding table and two chairs. Please let us know in advance if you require electricity. Feel free to bring handouts, giveaways etc.

Register today to showcase your organization and engage with our community's youth! Any questions please contact FarrinWagoner@gmail.com.

Sincerely,
The Glenmont PTA

Thank you for signing up to participate in the Bethlehem Activities Fair hosted by the Glenmont PTA! We appreciate your involvement and look forward to having you at the event.

Here are a few important details:

- **Date:** March 26, 2025
- **Location:** Glenmont Elementary School; 328 Rt 9W, Glenmont, NY 12077
- **Set-up Time:** 6:00 PM
- **Event Time:** 6:30-8 PM
- **Who It's For:** Families interested in activities for K-6 students, feel free to bring any swag, giveaways, sign up sheets, fliers etc

If you are a for-profit vendor, there is a **$20 participation fee**, which can be paid by **cash, check, or credit card** at the event.

Please let us know if you have any questions or need any assistance before the event. We're excited to have you join us and look forward to a great evening!

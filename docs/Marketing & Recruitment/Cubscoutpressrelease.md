Cub Scout Pack 232 to Host Fall Back to Scouting at Maple Ridge Park

GLENMONT - Cub Scout Pack 232 will host a
Fall Back to Scouting
event on Sunday, September 23, 2012 from 1:00
3:00 pm at Maple Ridge Park.

The event is open to boys in grades 1st
5th grade who attend Eagle and Glenmont Elementary who are interested in learning more about Cub Scouts. There will be games, treats, prizes and a rock climbing wall.  Siblings and families are welcome.

Throughout the year, Pack 232 participates in a wide-variety of events that promote the values of Cub Scouting and friendship.
Scouting activities include community service projects, visits to historical sites, camping and campfires, nature hikes, sporting contests, games, the Memorial
Day Parade, the ever popular Pinewood Derby and much, much more.

Leaders and current Cub Scouts will be there to answer questions and to explain more about the program.  Or, for more information, visit the Pack 232 website at:

http://www.pack232.com/

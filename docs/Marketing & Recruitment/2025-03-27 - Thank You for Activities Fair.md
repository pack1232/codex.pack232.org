On behalf of the Glenmont PTA, we want to extend our gratitude for your participation in our first-ever Activities Fair. Your presence and engagement made the event a success, and we appreciate the time and effort you put into connecting with potential new members.

We hope the evening was beneficial for your club, providing an opportunity to showcase what you do and encourage student involvement. *As this was our first time hosting this event, we would love to hear your feedback!* If you have any thoughts on what worked well, whether you found the event valuable, or any suggestions to help it run more smoothly in the future, please let us know. Your input will be invaluable in shaping future fairs and ensuring they continue to support our school community effectively.

Again, thank you for being part of this event. We appreciate your dedication to providing enriching opportunities for our students, and we *hope to see you again next year!*

| What are Cub Scouts About?
Scouting is a year round family centered program for boys AND girls in Kindergarten through 5th grade.  Scouting activities emphasize character development, citizenship, personal fitness and the outdoors while having fun!
How much does it cost?
1st year dues are $50.  Subsequent years are $95
Does my child need a uniform?

What is a Den meeting?
When your child and others in their grade group get together to do activities or go on an outing

When ALL dens and their families get together.

Right away!  Your den leader will contact you! |

## Slide 3: Scouting’s Mission

- To prepare young people to make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes by instilling in them the values of the Scout Oath and Law.
- “Fun with a Purpose”

## Slide 4: The Scout Oath

- On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.

## Slide 5: The Scout Law

- A Scout is trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent.

## Slide 6: The Outdoor Code

- As an American, I will do my best to –
- Be clean in my outdoor manners.
- Be careful with fire.
- Be considerate in the outdoors.
- Be conservation minded.

## Slide 15: Cub Scout Membership

- Boys and Girls join Pack 232
  - Pack 232 is chartered by the Selkirk Volunteer Fire Company #2
  - Pack 232 is part of the Fort Orange District in the Twin Rivers Council
  - Pack 232 was chartered in 1979 but can trace its history back over 85 years
  - Pack 232 has 47 active scouts and 12 adult leaders (all are BSA trained)
  - Pack 232 meets monthly on a Tuesday at the Glenmont Elementary School Cafeteria from 6:30-8pm
  - Meeting activities include: Gathering Activity/Craft, Opening, Special Guest, Game, Awards, Refreshments, and Closing
- Members are assigned to a den (same gender and generally same grade).
  - Meets once or twice a month

## Slide 16: Annual Registration Fees

- 1st Year Cub Scout Registration Fee - $50
  - Pack is Subsidizing Actual Cost
- Returning Cub Scout Registration Fee - $95
- Optional - Boy’s Life Magazine Subscription - $12
- There can be additional fees for outings
- There is financial support for families in need

## Slide 17: Fundraising Requirement - Popcorn!

- 1st Year Cub Scouts do NOT have to sell popcorn
- Returning Cub Scouts must sell:
  - $250 per Scout or
  - $350 per Scout family
  - However, there is an option to buy out:
    - Single Cub Scout - $100
    - 2 or more Cub Scouts in a family - $125 for the family
- Cub Scouts sell:
  - Online,
  - Door to door, and
  - Show and Sells
- Cub Scout Prizes!!!!:
  - Pack - Stewarts ice cream cone cert, trampoline outing, bowling outing and rock climbing outing
  - Council – Great Escape Lodge day
  - BSA – Amazon Gift Cards

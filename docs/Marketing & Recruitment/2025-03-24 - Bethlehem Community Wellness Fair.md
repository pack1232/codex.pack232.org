Good afternoon!

This year, the Bethlehem CSD Wellness Committee is organizing a Health and Wellness Fair to connect our families with various community resources in the areas of Health & Fitness, Sports & Recreation, Mindfulness & Stress Relief, Healthy Cooking & Nutrition with tips and more!

**Bethlehem Health and Wellness Fair**
Saturday, May 17, 2025
9:00 am - 1:00 pm
Bethlehem Central High School Gym A
(Gym doors open directly to the Delmar Farmer's Market)

Your organization will be provided with an area to display information, meet and greet families and promote services. There is no cost to participate. Space is limited so please reserve your spot promptly.

If you are interested in participating, please sign up here: [Health and Wellness Fair Exhibitor Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdPABGlbCNyNqMbe13LgRkVC5k4DjGpDxwPg7Jcide79hZQIA/viewform?usp=header). The deadline to reserve a table is Friday, May 2.

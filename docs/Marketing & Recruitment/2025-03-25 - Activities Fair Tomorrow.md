Hello!

Thank you again for registering for a table at our upcoming activities fair. We are expecting a good turnout! Some basic info:

- **Date:** March 26, 2025
- **Location:** Glenmont Elementary School **Cafeteria**; 328 Rt 9W, Glenmont, NY 12077, enter through main entrance and cafeteria is to the left; there will be signs
- **Set-up Time:** 6:00 PM - School's Out leaves at 6 pm, so **please don't arrive before 6**
- **Event Time:** 6:30-8 PM
- **Who It's For:** Families interested in activities for K-6 students, feel free to bring any swag, giveaways, sign up sheets, fliers etc

If you are a for-profit vendor, there is a **$20 participation fee**, which can be paid by **cash, check, or credit card** at the pizza table.

Please let us know if you have any questions or need any assistance before the event. We're excited to have you join us and look forward to a great evening! We will have plenty of extra chairs, tape and a few volunteers if you need a quick break!

Good afternoon everyone,

The Pack 232 Pinewood Derby is just two weeks away on Sunday, March 23rd at the Wire Event Center in Coxsackie! The organizing committee needs YOUR help to make this annual event a success.

No matter what you're interested in, we have a job for you: check-in, pit crew, race operator, taking pictures, decorating, judging cars, donating drinks or snacks, working the concession stand, setting up or breaking down. If you can help, we'd love to have you!

Please click the link below to get involved.

Event rules were sent out earlier in the week. We are racing under the same rules we have used in previous years. Please let me know if you have any questions about the upcoming race.

Thank you for your help with this event. We are looking forward to a great day of racing!

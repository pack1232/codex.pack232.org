Evening,
Below are some dates of events that we discussed last night during the leaders meeting. Richard, if there are any adjustments please let me know.

**Sunday Oct. 15th 10AM** Family Hike @ Peebles Island and Skit/Song rehearsal (cancel on heavy rain)

**Saturday Oct. 21st** Rocket Day at Camp Rotary see the calendar event in Scoutbook for additional details, Register independently via the Council Website.

**Sunday Oct. 22nd** Pack Meeting & Campfire at Kristy's Barn Afternoon to Dusk (will try to schedule a raindate)
There will be Apple picking and other activities available beforehand, we'll have a specific time to gather for a hayride to the campfire and serve apple cider and donuts with a campfire program.

**Wednesday Nov. 8th** Pack Meeting @ Glenmont Elementary Cafeteria
We have a tentative plan to have the Selkirk Fire Dept. come out for the scouts and we'll have the maps ready for Scouting for Food collection.

**Weekend of Nov. 11th** - Hang Door Hangars for Scouting for Food Time Day & Time TBD by individual Den Leaders

**Weekend of Nov. 18th** - Collect Scouting for Food donations. Finalizing date and time with Town soon.

**December 13th** Pack meeting at Glenmont Elementary Cafeteria - we'll hand out Pinewood Derby kits and have a festive Pie in the Face event for all the Popcorn sellers.

**March 8th-10th** Mark your Calendars for Cabin Camping @ Rotary. Always looking for parent volunteers to help with planning, let Richard or I know if you can commit and willing to help.

Any questions please feel free to reach out.

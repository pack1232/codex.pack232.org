Parents,

The registration for the USS Slater closes tomorrow. Late registrations will not be accepted. We currently have 47 Scouts and adults registered. There is a limit of 60 participants. All youth must have a completed Authorization Form submitted to me by 3/12/2025 -- that is tomorrow! I need to provide these forms to the USS Slater when finalizing our registration. Send me the form! The completed form can be sent to [cubmaster@pack232.com](mailto:cubmaster@pack232.com). To date I have only received 1 form. Get on it! I have attached the authorization form.

I've attached a list of scouts that have said that they will be attending.

The pack must pre-pay for this event. If you have registered then you must pay. Payment can be made during the pinewood derby on 3/23.

**USS Slater Overnight (Class A Uniform)**
**Friday April 4 - Saturday April 5, 2025**
**141 Broadway**
**Albany, NY 12202**

Join Pack 232 and spend the night on the USS Slater! The Pack will be sleeping over on the USS Slater 4/4/25 - 4/5/25. The cost is $50 per attendee, meals are included. The Pack will arrive at 5pm on 4/4 and depart by 9am on 4/5.

**Registration closes on 3/12/2025**. We cannot add participants after this date. Sign up today. All youth participants must have a completed Authorization Form submitted to the Pack by 3/12/2025. Please email the completed Authorization Forms to [cubmaster@pack232.com](mailto:cubmaster@pack232.com).

Please bring your registration payment to the Pinewood Derby on 3/23/25. Payment can be made in cash or check payable to Pack 232. As we are pre-paying for this event, if you have registered you must pay.

Optional patches and dog tags are available for purchase. Patches are $3 and dog tags $5. Orders and payment for these optional items will be taken at the Pinewood Derby on 3/23/2025.

Mike McGuire
Assistant Cubmaster

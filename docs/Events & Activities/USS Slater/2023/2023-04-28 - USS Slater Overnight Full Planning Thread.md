## Planning Timeline

### January 4, 2023 - Initial Booking

Shanna Schuster (USS Slater) confirmed booking for Saturday, May 6, 2023. A deposit from 2020 was credited to the event. Reservation form and overnight information provided.

**Key policies:**
- Minimum 20 campers (can bring 10 but must pay for 20)
- $100 deposit applied; final payment due 3 weeks prior
- USS SLATER or DEHM must be listed as certificate holder on insurance

### March 8, 2023 - Tour Options & Pricing

Kristen Crumb inquired about adding tour-only options for families not staying overnight.

**Pricing confirmed:**
- **Tour only:** $7/adult, $5/child
- **Tour + educational programming + dinner (no overnight):** $25/person
- **Overnight:** $50/person

Initial headcount: 12 scouts and 10 adults for overnight.

### April 24-28, 2023 - Finalizing Headcount

Kristen reached out to finalize numbers. Sign-up issues between Scoutbook, SignUpGenius, and Facebook complicated the count.

**April 27:** Josh McWilliam informed USS Slater they expected to finalize that evening. Kristen mentioned tracking down confirmations across multiple platforms.

**April 28 - Final Count & Payment:**

Kristen submitted updated totals:
- Overnight: 16 adults & 23 youth
- 25 patches, 5 dog tags

**Final cost: $1,987.00**
- 16 adults & 23 youth overnight = $1,850
- 1 tour + program + dinner = $25
- 1 adult & 1 child tour only = $12
- 25 patches = $75
- 5 dog tags = $25

USS Slater requested immediate payment to order food and make dog tags in time.

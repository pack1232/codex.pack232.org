Hello,

Thank you for visiting the USS SLATER with your group last season. It is thanks to the generosity of people like you that we have been able to make the SLATER a living, breathing tribute to the sacrifices of all our nation's veterans.

USS SLATER is about to open for our 27th season in Albany! It is a perfect time to book your overnight or tour before schedules get too hectic!

**2024 Season:**
- Opens Wednesday, April 3rd through Sunday, December 1st
- Tours: Wednesday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
- Also open Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, and Veterans Day
- Overnights available any night of the week (most groups prefer Friday or Saturday)

**Group Rates (10+ people, booked in advance):**
- Adults: $8.00
- Students: $6.00
- Overnights: $50 per person (regardless of age, minimum 20 people)

**Contact:** 518-431-1943 or mulligan@ussslater.org

Jo Ann Mulligan
Business Manager, USS SLATER

Folks,

It was nice seeing so many of you at our Pack meeting last night. As promised here is some additional information about summer camp. If you have any questions at all please do not hesitate to ask! My family has been to overnight camp the past 2 summers and day camp for the past 3 summers. My kids have really enjoyed their time at camp! Twin Rivers Council does offer camperships for those needing a little help getting to camp.

If you are planning to go to resident (overnight) camp let me know so I can sign you up! We need to register as a Pack to ensure we are all together.

## Cub Scout Summer Resident Camp (Optional)

**Session 1 - Sunday July 26, 2026 - Wednesday July 29, 2026**
**Rotary Scout Reservation**
**279 Davitt Lake Rd, Averill Park, NY 12018**

Pack 232 will be attending summer resident (sleepover) camp at Rotary Scout Reservation. The Pack will be camping during Session 1 - July 26-29, 2026. Summer camp is an exciting and exhausting four days and three nights. Scouts get to participate in a lot of awesome activities.

Details about camp can be found here: [https://rsrbsa.org/cubcamp/](https://rsrbsa.org/cubcamp/)

Camp is co-ed and all Scouts entering ranks Tiger-AOL in September 2026 are able to attend camp. All scouts must attend with their parent/guardian. Only one parent is required for those families with multiple scouts.

Scouts must be registered as part of our Pack. I am serving as our pack reservation contact and will be adding you and your scout to our camp roster. **If you want to attend summer camp please email me with your name and your scout's name. I will add you to the roster and send you a link that can be used to pay and update details for you and your scout.**

If you plan to attend please let me know by 5/15. This will allow you to take advantage of the discounted registration fee. Registrations after 5/15 will be accepted. Camp does fill up, please secure your spot now!

### 2026 Resident Camp Fees

- Cub Scout Regular Fee - $275 (Paid by June 1st)
- Cub Scout Late Fee - $325 (Paid after June 1st)
- Adult Participant Fee - $100
- Den Chief Fee - $100

Payment does not need to be made at registration.

*Twin Rivers Council offers a campership program for those families that might need a little financial help with the registration fee. I will share campership details once they become available.*

## Cub Scout Summer Day Camp (Optional)

**Monday July 20, 2026 - Friday July 24, 2026**
**Rotary Scout Reservation**
**279 Davitt Lake Rd, Averill Park, NY 12018**

Cub Scout Day Camp is available at Camp Rotary. Transportation is available for an additional fee. Please note that day camp is available at all three Twin Rivers Council camps. Make sure to select the correct camp when registering! Detailed information can be found here:

[https://rsrbsa.org/cub-scout-day-camp/](https://rsrbsa.org/cub-scout-day-camp/)

### 2026 Day Camp Fees

- Cub Scout Youth - $250
- Cub Scout Youth with Parent Volunteer - $175
- Additional Cub Scout in the Family Attending - $175
- Optional transportation - $75

**Camperships Available**

Dear Scouting Families,

We're excited for another incredible summer of adventure at our Twin Rivers Council camps! Whether you're planning to attend Cub Scout Day Camp, Cub Scout Resident Camp, or Scouts BSA Resident Camp, we want to make sure that every Scout has the opportunity to experience the fun, learning, and outdoor excitement that camp has to offer.

If financial circumstances present a challenge, camperships are available to help cover a portion of the camp fees. These camperships are awarded based on need and are designed to supplement fundraising efforts and personal contributions.

**How to Apply for a Campership:**

1. Complete the Campership Application -- The form is available on the Twin Rivers Council website
2. Submit Before the Deadline -- Applications should be submitted by April 1st for best consideration
3. Stay Tuned for Notification -- Approved camperships will be communicated via email before camp begins.

We encourage all Scouts to take advantage of this opportunity if needed. No Scout should miss out on camp due to financial concerns! If you have any questions or need assistance with the application, please reach out to your unit leader or the council office.

Yours in Scouting,
TRC Camping Team

Parents,

Pack 232 will be attending summer resident (sleepover) camp at Rotary Scout Reservation. The Pack will be camping during Session 1 - July 27-30, 2025. Summer camp is an exciting and exhausting four days and three nights. Scouts get to participate in a lot of awesome activities, including:

- Aquatics: swimming and boating
- Shooting Sports: archery, slingshots and BBs
- Crafts
- Nature Hikes and Environmental Science
- STEM
- Sports and Games
- First Aid
- Scout Skills
- Fishing
- Bouldering
- Drinking Slush Puppies (probably their favorite activity)
- Campfire Skits and Songs
- More Slush Puppies

All meals are provided by the RSR dining hall. Food allergies and sensitivities are taken seriously and are accommodated for. The Trading Post is open for all of those camp necessities (shirts, cups, snacks, etc.). The pack will camp as a unit at one of the many RSR campsites. We will be using their platform tents and cots. Electricity for charging devices and CPAP machines is available.

More information: https://rsrbsa.org/cubcamp/

**Registration Information:**

Camp is co-ed and all Scouts entering ranks Tiger-AOL in September 2025 are able to attend camp. All scouts must attend with their parent/guardian. Only one parent is required for those families with multiple scouts.

Scouts must be registered as part of our Pack. If you want to attend summer camp please email Mike with your name and your scout's name.

If you plan to attend please let me know by 5/15. This will allow you to take advantage of the discounted registration fee. Camp does fill up, please secure your spot now!

**2025 Resident Camp Fees:**

- Cub Scout Regular Fee - $275 (Paid by June 1st)
- Cub Scout Late Fee - $325 (Paid after June 1st)
- Adult Participant Fee - $100
- Den Chief Fee - $100

Payment does not need to be made at registration.

*Twin Rivers Council offers a campership program for those families that might need a little help with the registration fee.*

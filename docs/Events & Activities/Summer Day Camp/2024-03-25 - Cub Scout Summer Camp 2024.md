Parents,

Registration for summer camp at Rotary Scout Reservation (RSR) is currently open. RSR offers Cub Scout Day Camp and Cub Scout Resident (overnight) Camp.

**RESIDENT CAMP**

The Pack will be attending Resident Camp **Session 1**, Sunday 7/28/2024 1:00pm through Wednesday 7/31/2024 10:00am. This year's theme is Magic Forest. The cost per Scout is $240 & $95 per adult participant. Registration: https://scoutingevent.com/364-70516#

General information: https://rsrbsa.org/cubcamp/

For planning purposes please register by 5/20/2024. If you register by 3/31/2024 you can pay a $100 deposit per Scout with the balance due 6/5/2024. Adult participants must pay in full at registration.

When completing registration use the Select Unit button and identify Pack 1232 as your unit!

All adults attending Resident Camp are required to complete the BSA Youth Protection Training: https://my.scouting.org/

**DAY CAMP**

RSR Day Camp will be held 8/12/2024 through 8/16/2024. This year's theme is Wild Wild West. Cost per Scout is $180. Optional bus available for $66 (pickup/dropoff at Delmar Hannaford and/or Colonie Center Macy's).

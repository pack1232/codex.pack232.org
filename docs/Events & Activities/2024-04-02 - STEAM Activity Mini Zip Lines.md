FYI - There are still a few openings left at the Tech Valley Center of Gravity STEAM activity tomorrow. Registration will be open until 10:00am tomorrow.

A Twin Rivers Council activity for Cub Scouts was just added. STEAM Studio at Tech Valley Center of Gravity at 30 3rd St, in Troy is offering a mini zip line activity - participants will be challenged to create designs that get your miniature dinosaur toys across the zip lines safely. Experiment with different designs, and see how you can make your zip line cart move even faster!

- Two time slots available on April 3rd: 10:00-12:00 & 1:00-3:00
- Both sessions limited to 20 slots
- Cost per scout: $18 (3% fee for credit card transactions)
- Register: https://scoutingevent.com/364-83575-200489#

Registration begins at 9:00 am
To speed registration and avoid long lines, please register in advance.
We’ll have a full day of Cub Scout camping fun and
Activities at three separate locations: Camp Bedford, Rotary Scout
Reservation and Camp Wakpominee. Choose the location that is
convenient for you. Activities vary by location but will include:
archery, crafts, spooky games, and other cool stuff! Bring the
whole family.
This will be a great kick-off to your Pack’s program!
Interested Packs can include a Family Camping Weekend with
Harvest Fun Day as their program on Saturday. Site Reservations need to
be made separately online.
Register Today!
Youth Registration $5.00 (prior to 10/4) (includes Program and Patch)
Youth Registration walk-in $10.00 (includes Program and Patch)
Adult Registration $0
Optional Patch $3.00
You choose your lunch option:
Bring your own— $0 additional fee
Concession Stand - Hamburgers and hot dogs available. Prices are a la carte
Dining Hall Lunch: Additional $5.00
These events will be held rain or shine.
Please make your reservation prior to October 4th, 2017
Register online at: www.trcscouting.org

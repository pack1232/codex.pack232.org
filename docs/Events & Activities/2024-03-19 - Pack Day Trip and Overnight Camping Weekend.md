Hey folks,

Mike McGuire came up with a fantastic suggestion for a Pack day trip... Cooperstown Farmers Museum! Other than the Pack meeting, we don't have anything planned for April. What are your thoughts on going there Sunday, April 21st? The hours are 10:00 am - 4:00 pm. Website: https://www.farmersmuseum.org/

For a Pack overnight camping trip, Yankee Doodle district is hosting a spring camporee, titled "Frontier Challenge", at Rotary Scout Reservation on May 17-19, 2024. We can go as a Pack and stay overnight Friday & Saturday. Prices are $8.00 for Cub Scout youth and $10.00 for Cub Scout adults. Of course, we would have to add more on top of that to pay for the food. Families would have to bring their own tents, but the leaders would buy and prepare the food.

For more specific information, see the attached Leaders Guide or go to the TRC event details page: https://scoutingevent.com/364-82628-198679

What are your thoughts on these two activities?

Yours in Scouting,
Richard

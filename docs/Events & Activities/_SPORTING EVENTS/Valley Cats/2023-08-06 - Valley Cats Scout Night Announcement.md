Evening Parents,

I hope you and your scouts have been having a great summer. Pack and Den Leaders will be starting up planning and communications for the upcoming Fall soon, if you know or have access to a unique location, experience, or other facility that could host or visit a Den or Pack meeting please let us know (Cubmaster@Pack232.com) and I'll assist in coordinating with a specific Den Leader or help organize a Pack activity. If you have a great idea that you want to propose for a Scouting activity also feel free to reach out.

This coming Saturday join the Pack and other scouts for **Scout Night with the Valley Cats @ "The Joe"**. Please RSVP on this calendar event and I'll purchase a block of tickets on Thursday so that scouts can sit together. Put the total number of tickets needed in the Comment box if additional parents/siblings/guests are attending.

- **Game tickets**: $14/person for Reserved section 270
- **Camping option**: $17/person (sleep over in the field after the game) - includes Reserved Box Ticket, Hot Dog & Soda Voucher, and $2 Donation to Twin Rivers Council. Email cubmaster directly if interested.

Scouts who would like to be a part of the parade around the field before the start of the game should be in **Class A uniform** and ready to line-up at the left field foul pole just before 7PM.

**Event Details:**
- Calendar: Pack 1232
- Event: Scout Night at Joe Bruno Stadium, Valley Cats vs. New Jersey Jackals
- Where: The Joe, 80 Vandenburgh Ave, Troy, NY 12180
- When: Aug 12, 2023, 7pm - 10pm

Parents,

Attached is the attendee guide for our Winter Cabin Camping trip! We still have space for those looking to attend. The cost to attend is $10 per person.

Please RSVP on Scoutbook by 1/25/26!

In addition to personal gear we ask that each family bring the following items:

- 1 bundle of firewood
- Reusable water bottles and mugs - the Pack will NOT be providing cups
- A snack to share - chips, cookies, crackers, etc. This does not have to be enough for everyone - if we all bring 1 thing then there will be plenty to go around.
- Sled
- Helmets for your scout if they will be sledding!

There is a lot of information in the attendee guide. Please let me know if you have any questions or concerns. As we get closer to the event I will be sending out cabin assignments.

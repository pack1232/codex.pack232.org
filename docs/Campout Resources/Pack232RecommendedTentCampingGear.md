# Recommended Gear

- Tent
- Duffel bag or backpack
- Medication, if needed, with current dosage/instruction label and Cub Scout's name
- Inhaler or bee-sting kit, if needed
- Cub Scout Handbook
- Sleeping bag (Extra blankets are fine to supplement warmth of sleeping bag if necessary.)
- Pillow
- Mattress pad - foam or air pad
- Folding chair
- Poncho or Rain suit (Inexpensive Wal-Mart special works just fine in a pinch.)
- Insect repellent (No aerosol cans.)
- Sunscreen (No aerosol cans.) and Sunglasses
- Hat
- Paper and pen
- Flashlight (check batteries and bring extras) (Headlamp works best but handheld is fine, too.)
- Mess Kit -- (a plastic bowl, plate and cup, plus fork and spoon work just fine.)
- Water bottle (Plastic or metal; no glass.)
- Hooded Sweatshirt
- Wind Breaker
- Pocket Knife - Only cub scouts who have earned their whittling chip (Bears, Webelos, and Arrow of Light) may carry a pocket knife
- Socks x 2
- Underwear x2
- Short-sleeve shirt x2 (especially, the Pack 232 Class "B" T-shirt)
- Shorts x2
- Long pants or Sweatpants
- Swimsuit
- Hiking Boots
- Extra sneakers/shoes (no open toed shoes)
- Sleep clothes/pajamas
- Toothbrush, Toothpaste and Floss
- Towel
- Ice chest for food
- Face masks
- Hand sanitizer

# Optional Gear

- Watch
- Binoculars
- Compass
- Cards, games, frisbee
- Personal first aid kit (we have a Pack first aid kit)
- Wagon to tow gear
- Fishing pole
- Hammock

Grant Request: Pinewood Derby Timing & Software Upgrade for Cub Scout Pack 232
Dear Selkirk Community Fund Grant Committee,
On behalf of Cub Scout Pack 232, I am writing to request a grant of $500 to upgrade the outdated timing and computer system used for our annual Pinewood Derby. While our physical track remains in good condition, the laptop and software used to run the event have become unreliable, creating significant challenges during our most recent derby.
The Pinewood Derby is more than just a race—it's an entry point into engineering, physics, and data analysis for our Scouts. Participants design, build, and refine their cars while learning core STEM concepts such as:
Aerodynamics
Friction and weight distribution
Measurement and precision
Iterative design and testing
Our current system struggles to accurately track heat times, organize race brackets, and display real-time results, limiting the educational and engagement potential of the event. With your support, we plan to purchase a modern laptop and updated timing software that will ensure accurate data tracking, smoother operation, and a more interactive STEM experience for our Scouts and their families.
This small but meaningful investment will have a long-lasting impact on our pack, allowing us to continue offering high-quality STEM-based programming that sparks curiosity, critical thinking, and a love of learning in our young participants.
Thank you for considering our request and for supporting hands-on STEM education in the community.
Sincerely,

Josh McWilliam
Committee Chair, Cub Scout Pack 232 (a.k.a 1232)

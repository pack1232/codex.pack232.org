Attendees: Josh McWilliam, Mike McGuire, Bob Baldwin, Rob Pattison, Richard Crumb

Notes taken by Josh McWilliam:

Popcorn
Josh to contact Ben to send Popcorn email out to troop.
We will encourage new scouts/families to sell popcorn by communicating the general expectations, the need and fact that all activities funded by this. Will welcome donation in lieu of popcorn, but will not require it as we know the first year can be an expensive one (uniforms, etc.).
Need to sync with Ben on this fact per his email so he doesn’t contract it.
Communicate this at the Pack meetings, starting with the September meeting, really need to communicate the importance of popcorn sales and signing up for show and sells. Also possible need to add Pack dues if unsuccessful which we would like to avoid.
Still like idea of leaderboard to encourage families to sell, perhaps teams leaderboards too. Concern is how much effort that is and likely not something Ben has time for. Need to ask Ben if there is a way to easily get the data out of Trails End as there may be an easy way to construct a leaderboard from an export if it enables it.
Per Mike, need more show and sells. Josh to email Ben Friedman and Dave Roberts (Troop 75 Popcorn Kernal) for guidance on that.
Discussed the idea of a “scout account” where the pack will hold money for scouts in reserve that they earn that they can then use to pay for scout activities. Troop 75 does this (see policy here). Our proposal is that once a scout sells twice the minimum popcorn amount, we would split the proceeds between the Pack and the Scout Account 50/50. In a world where we have super sellers this could hurt the Pack, but that doesn’t means its fair to those super sellers and we don’t have any now anyway. If scout crosses over to a Troop with a scout account system and they want to take the funds with them we would transfer it into that scouts Troop scout account, otherwise it would go to Pack 232 general funds.
Josh to contact Ken and cc Bob on how they track this in Quickbooks Online for Troop 75

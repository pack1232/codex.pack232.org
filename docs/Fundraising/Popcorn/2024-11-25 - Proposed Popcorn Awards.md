Hi Everyone,

I did some looking around and have the following proposals for the inaugural Popcorn Kernel Cup awards. The following will cost about $100 in total awards.

## Winning Team Members

For each person on the Popcorn Kernel Cup winning team (Team Kettle Corn currently) that sold popcorn, they would get an actual popcorn kernel cup with their name on it.

Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/961381688/personalized-popcorn-bucketreusable

**Estimated total cost:** $40-50

I had originally envisioned a trophy of some sort for this but it dawned on me that a single trophy for a team of popcorn sellers can well be shared and ultimately wouldn't then motivate kids as much, so I came up with the above idea.

## Team Top Sellers

For the top seller on each of the 4 teams we would give an actual trophy.

Source: https://www.crownawards.com/Star_Stream_Insert_Trophy.Trophies.Star_Stream_Insert_Trophy/TRSSHDG.html

**Estimated Cost:** $30

## All Team + Den Top Sellers

For the top sellers of each team and/or den (lots of overlap there so doesn't amount to all that many) we would give one of the following top popcorn seller patches:

Source: https://tradingpost.classb.com/popcorn-top-seller/

**Estimated Cost:** $30

If I move soon I believe I could get them all in time for the next Pack Meeting.

What do you think?

YIS,
Josh

Pack 232 Parents,

Happy Halloween!

November shaping up to be a great month for Pack 232!

Additional event details are available on Scoutbook. Please check Scoutbook for the most up to date information.

**Fundraising:**

As you know Pack 232 subsidizes our activities with money raised through the sale of popcorn. There are plenty of popcorn shifts remaining. Please take a look at the Trails End app to register for open shifts.

There is still time for you to use the paper order form and take orders from friends and family. Order forms need to be submitted by Sunday 11/3. Order forms can be dropped off to Ben Friedman at 8 Somerset Dr, Glenmont, NY 12077 on Sunday 11/3 between 10am-2pm.

The latest Pack 232 Popcorn Kernel Cup standing show team Kettle Corn out front with a total of $2,635 raised! Keep up the good work!

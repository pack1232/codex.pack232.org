Folks,

Happy 4th of July!

The Pack held our annual planning meeting last weekend. During the meeting we developed our 2025/2026 Pack calendar of events. I have attached a copy of the calendar for your reference. Events, dates, and times are subject to change.

As noted last month we will be transitioning the scouts to their next den level. I would encourage you take a look at the elective adventures for your new rank. Scouts can complete most adventures independently of their dens and there are some that may line up with your summer plans!

Adventures can be found here: https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/

## National Night Out (Optional)

**Tuesday August 5, 2025 5:00-8:00pm**
**Elm Ave Park**
**261 Elm Ave, Delmar, NY 12054**

Enjoy this fun and free event! National Night Out is one of the largest crime prevention gatherings in the US. Information about crime prevention and personal safety will be provided by many different organizations. Activities include: music, poster contest, bounce house, fire truck displays and demonstrations, ambulance and EMS equipment displays, police vehicles, free ice cream, raffles and more.

Stop by the Pack 232 table and say hello!

## Muddy Kids Family Obstacle Mud Run (Optional)

**Saturday August 23, 2025 9:00am**
**Harley Rendezvous Classic Inc.**
**1142 Batter St, Pattersonville, NY 12137**

THIS EVENT WAS RESCHEDULED TO AUGUST 23rd!

Join Pack 232 and participate in the Muddy Kids Family Obstacle Mud Run! Muddy Kids is a 1.5-2.2 mile adventure event series for families and kids ages 5-15, with 15+ fun and challenging obstacles, mud and entertainment for the whole family. Registration is $39 per person plus tax & fees - works out to about $45 per person.

Register at: https://muddykids.us/events/albany-ny-2/

Please register for the 9:30am time slot. They seem to post coupon codes on their facebook page, check there to see if you can save some $.

## September Pack Meeting / Fall Back to Scouting (Class B Uniform)

**Sunday September 14, 2025 2:00pm-5:00pm**
**Selkirk Fire House #2**
**301 Glenmont Rd, Glenmont, NY 12077**
Flag Ceremony - AOLs

Our first meeting of the 2025-2026 scouting year will be at Selkirk Fire House #2. We will be meeting outside behind the firehouse. We have a variety of events planned, including: an egg drop, inflatable archery range, ice cream, etc.

This meeting will double as a recruitment event. Please bring any friends that might be interested in joining!

## Thacher Park Family Hike (Class B Uniform)

**Saturday September 20, 2025 2:00pm**
**Thacher State Park**
**830 Thacher Park Rd, Voorheesville, NY 12186**

Weather permitting, please join the whole Pack for a fun family hike. We will be hiking the Indian Ladder Trail. Please make sure to dress appropriately and don't forget the 6 essentials: first aid kit, water bottle, sun protection, snack, emergency whistle, & flashlight. We will meet in the visitor parking lot.

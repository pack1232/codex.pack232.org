Cubmaster’s Minute
 At times, you may think things are tough especially when you are
asked to do chores and your parents/teachers correct you. But if
you think about it for a moment, you should realize you have a good
life. You have food, a home to live/sleep in, a school to go to learn,
and you have your friends. You also have a family who loves you.
I am sure your family does lots of Good Turns for you that you may
not think about.
 In November, we’re going to do a Good Turn for two reasons:
 1. The Scout Oath pledges us to help other people at all times.
 2. We owe something to those around us – our parents and our
community.
 I expect to see all of you out on November 9/10 and 16 for Scouting
for Food not because you have to but because you want to.
 If you do not get this food, people in our community will have less to
eat.

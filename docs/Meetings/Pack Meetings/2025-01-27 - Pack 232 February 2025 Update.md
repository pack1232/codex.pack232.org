Pack 232 Parents,

Happy February. There is a lot of information here. We have a variety of upcoming events that require an RSVP. If you are planning to attend any of the following activities please RSVP on Scoutbook ASAP!

- Snow Tubing - 2/15
- Winter Cabin Camping - 3/7-9
- USS Slater Overnight - 4/4

Please note that dates for summer camp are available. Twin Rivers Council organizes two summer camps; resident (overnight) camp, and day camp. If you are like me then you are already planning your summer camps... Please consider scout camp. My kids have been to resident and day camp. Please contact me with any questions.

Additional event details are available on Scoutbook. Please check Scoutbook for the most up to date information.

### Pack Snow Tubing (Outdoor Gear)

**Saturday February 15, 2025 Time TBD**
**78 Catamount Rd, Hillsdale, NY 12529**
**Catamount Mountain**

***Please RSVP on Scoutbook by Thursday, February 6th so we could give the resort a final count. If you are having trouble with the Scoutbook RSVP text me at 518-944-7126.***

Join Pack 232 for snow tubing at Catamount Mountain Resort. The cost is $29 per person at the group rate (15 people or more are required for this).

There is a separate parking lot for the snow tubing so be on the lookout for the sign. Bathrooms, fire pits, and food truck all on premises.

### NOVA Science Everywhere for Cub Scouts (Class B Uniform)

**Friday February 21, 2025 9:30am - 12:30pm**
**High Rollers Roller Skating Rink**
**4313 NY-30, Amsterdam NY, 12010**

Twin Rivers Council is organizing the NOVA Science Everywhere event for Cub Scouts at High Rollers Roller Skating Arena. Scouts will participate in fun hands on STEM activities that center on roller skating. After attending the NOVA Science Everywhere event scouts can complete a few additional requirements at home and earn their NOVA Science Everywhere Award.

Registration: https://scoutingevent.com/364-novacubscouts0225

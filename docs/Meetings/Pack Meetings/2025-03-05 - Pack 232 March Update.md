Pack 232 Parents,

Welcome to March! This year has flown by. As always there is a lot of information here.

Please note that we will not be having a March Pack meeting at Glenmont Elementary School. Awards will be distributed at the Pine Wood Derby on 3/23.

We have a variety of upcoming events that require an RSVP. If you are planning to attend any of the following activities please RSVP on Scoutbook!

- USS Slater Overnight - 4/4
- Pack Spring Campout with Troop 75 at Thompson's Lake Campground - 5/16-5/18

**Registration for the USS Slater Trip closes on 3/12/2025. Please RSVP now!**

Please note that dates for summer camp are available. Twin Rivers Council organizes two summer camps; resident (overnight) camp, and day camp. Please consider scout camp. Please contact me with any questions.

***Twin Rivers Council offers a campership program for those families that might need a little help with the registration fee. The application can be accessed on the Rotary Scout Reservation homepage.***

https://rsrbsa.org/

Additional event details are available on Scoutbook. Please check Scoutbook for the most up to date information.

### USS Slater Overnight (Class A Uniform)

**Friday April 4 - Saturday April 5, 2025**
**141 Broadway, Albany, NY 12202**

Join Pack 232 and spend the night on the USS Slater! The Pack will be sleeping over on the USS Slater 4/4/25 - 4/5/25. The cost is $50 per attendee, meals are included. The Pack will arrive at 5pm on 4/4 and depart by 9am on 4/5.

**Registration closes on 3/12/2025**. We cannot add participants after this date. Sign up today. All youth participants must have a completed Authorization Form submitted to the Pack by 3/12/2025. Please email the completed Authorization Forms to cubmaster@pack232.com.

Please bring your registration payment to the Pinewood Derby on 3/23/25. Payment can be made in cash or check payable to Pack 232. As we are pre-paying for this event, if you have registered you must pay.

Optional patches and dog tags are available for purchase. Patches are $3 and dog tags $5. Orders and payment for these optional items will be taken at the Pinewood Derby on 3/23/2025.

### Cub Scout Summer Resident Camp (Optional)

**Session 1 - Sunday July 27, 2025 - Wednesday July 30, 2025**
**Rotary Scout Reservation**
**279 Davitt Lake Rd, Averill Park, NY 12018**

Pack 232 will be attending summer resident (sleepover) camp at Rotary Scout Reservation. The Pack will be camping during Session 1 - July 27-30, 2025. Summer camp is an exciting and exhausting four days and three nights. Scouts get to participate in a lot of awesome activities.

Details about camp: https://rsrbsa.org/cubcamp/

Camp is co-ed and all Scouts entering ranks Tiger-AOL in September 2025 are able to attend camp. All scouts must attend with their parent/guardian. Only one parent is required for those families with multiple scouts.

Scouts must be registered as part of our Pack. **If you want to attend summer camp please email me with your name and your scout's name.** Please let me know by 5/15 to take advantage of the discounted registration fee.

**2025 Resident Camp Fees:**
- Cub Scout Regular Fee - $275 (Paid by June 1st)
- Cub Scout Late Fee - $325 (Paid after June 1st)
- Adult Participant Fee - $100
- Den Chief Fee - $100

Payment does not need to be made at registration.

If you are a new scout family your scout can receive 1 free Class B shirt upon sign up. Additional shirts & adult shirts are $16 - cash or check.

## Monster Mashoree - Twin Rivers Council Fall Camporee (Optional Event, Class B Uniform)

**Friday October 10-12, 2025**
**Schaghticoke Fairgrounds**
**69 Stillwater Bridge Rd, Schaghticoke, NY 12154**

Once every 2 years the Twin Rivers Council organizes a council camporee. This year's theme is Monster Mash hence Monster Mashoree. The Mashoree will be held 10/10-10/12 at the Schaghticoke Fairgrounds. This event will pull in scouts from across the region and is a massive event that you don't want to miss. Activities will include: Drone Show, Archery, BB gun shooting, Slingshots, Trade-O-Ree (think scout store but way bigger), Costume Contest, Face Painting, Magic the Gathering Tournament (for the Cubs too), RC Race Cars, Lego Pinewood Derby, Reptile Show, Planetarium, Video Game Trailer, Bounce Houses, Climbing Wall, NOVA Award Activities, Live Band, Movie, and more. There will not be enough time to experience it all!

The Pack will be organizing a cohort to attend this event. We will be camping Friday & Saturday night. The event registration is $55 per youth and $45 per adult. Registration includes admission, an event patch, a deck of Monster Mashoree Playing Cards, and all meals & coffee (Saturday night dinner will be Chicken BBQ provided by the event). The Pack will be organizing food for Saturday breakfast, lunch, and Sunday breakfast. There is a Saturday only day option available for $50 per youth and $40 per adult. **This registration price includes the event registration and additional food not provided by the event.**

Scouts will need a parent/guardian to attend with them. Limited camping supplies are available to borrow - let me know if you need something.

We will be registering for this event as a Pack. Regular price registration closes on 9/30. Please RSVP on Scoutbook no later than 9/29.

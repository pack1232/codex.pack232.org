## Upcoming Events

### December Pack Meeting (Class A Uniform)

**Thursday December 12, 2024 6:30pm-7:30pm**
**Glenmont Elementary School**
**328 Rt 9W, Glenmont, NY 12077**
Flag Ceremony - Tigers

Join Pack 232 at our December Pack meeting, this is not one to miss! The pack will be recognizing our Scouts for their fantastic effort in selling popcorn this year. The winners of the inaugural Popcorn Kernel Cup will be announced! As you may know scouts that sell $500 of popcorn get to throw a pie into the face of the leader of their choice. Our leaders have been warned, pies will be flying at the December meeting. If your scout was a little short of the $500 goal do not fret. Parents have the option to pay the lesser of $20 or the difference to bring the total sales up to $500. By doing so your kiddo can throw a pie too! (Cash or check only)

Also at this meeting the Pack will be handing out pinewood derby cars / Lego cars to the kids. The Pinewood Derby is slated for March. We are working to finalize the date. Lion Scouts will receive a Lego car and all other scouts will receive a pinewood derby car.

### First Day Hike (Outdoor Gear)

**Wednesday January 1, 2025 10:00am**
**Five Rivers Environmental Education Center**
**56 Game Farm Rd, Delmar, NY 12054**

Join Pack 232 and ring in the New Year with a New Year's Day hike! Dust off those New Year's Eve cobwebs with a hike at Five Rivers. Join the pack in hiking the Vlomankill Trail (0.6 mile) and the Beaver Tree Trail (0.5 mile). This event is weather permitting, if we have to cancel an email will be sent out to the whole pack.

Please dress for the weather and don't forget the 6 Cub Scout Essentials!

### The Amazing Cub Yukon (Outdoor Gear)

**Saturday January 11, 2025 8:30am-3:30pm**
**Lawson Lake County Park**
**293 Lawson Lake Rd, Feura Bush, NY 12067**

Pack 232 will be participating in The Amazing Cub Yukon. This Twin Rivers Council event focuses on winter outdoor activities. Please dress for the weather, plan to be outside for much of the day, and bring some snacks! The registration cost is $5 per scout and adult. Coffee, Hot Cocoa, and Ramen are included.

Please RSVP in Scout Book **and** register on the Twin Rivers Council website. The registration fee can be paid online when register by credit card (+ 3% convenience fee) or via check mailed to Twin Rivers Council. When registering please identify your unit as Pack 1232 Family.

Registration: https://scoutingevent.com/364-AmazingCubYukon

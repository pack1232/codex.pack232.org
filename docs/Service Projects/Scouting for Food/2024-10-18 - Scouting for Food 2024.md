Leaders,

Attached are the Scouting for Food canvass maps for our 2024 food collection. We will be canvassing the neighborhoods and hanging the door hangers on 11/9/2024. Food collection is 11/16/2024. Your canvass area may have changed since last year so please make sure you look at the maps. If you would like to cover additional areas please let me know, we can always add new territory.

Please coordinate with your den and organize your meeting location and times.

This year our food will be donated to the **Venture Churches Food Pantry** located at 38 Church Rd, Selkirk, NY 12158. Food can be dropped off at the food pantry between 11:00am-12:00 noon on 11/16/2024. Please schedule your Den's 11/16 food collection so that you can drop the food off between 11:00am-12:00 noon.

We will have door hangers available the week before our canvassing.

Please let me know if you have any questions.

Mike McGuire
Assistant Cubmaster

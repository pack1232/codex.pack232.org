Welcome to Pack 1232 Selkirk Volunteer Fire Company No 2. We are excited you have joined to be a part of our exciting Scouting Adventures.

Unless otherwise specified all Pack (group) meetings are on the second Thursday of the month from 6:30pm-7:30pm. Meetings are held at the Glenmont Elementary School Cafeteria. Glenmont Elementary is located at 328 Route 9W, Glenmont, NY 12077.

Each Scout also meets with the Den based on your grade and gender. These are typically held once per month. Your Den Leader will be reaching out to you soon.

Our website (pack232.com) contains many resources that will help answer many of your questions. For the safety and privacy of our scouts and their families we keep some of the information on this site password protected. The password to access these pages is "doyourbest", which is the Cub Scout motto.

Here are some specific resources from the site you may find useful:

- Parent FAQs: https://www.pack232.com/parent-faq/
- Leadership: https://www.pack232.com/leadership/
- Den Information: https://www.pack232.com/dens/

Here is a link to our event calendar for this year:

https://drive.google.com/file/d/12UCgqqWvOSkwJUhmn_XKbCh8LK5WGE4T/view?usp=drive_link

You will be able to track your scout’s progress and view with the Pack and Den calendars and receive email announcements through Scoutbook. Setting up an account with Scoutbook should be one of your first steps, although you may want to wait till tomorrow to do so as it sometimes takes overnight for the application system to push your information over to Scoutbook. Check out the following resource tomorrow to get setup:

https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/scoutbook-tutorial-for-parents/

Please feel free to reach out to me or any of the leaders noted on the site, particularly the Den Leader for your scout's grade.

We look forward to having you as a member of our Pack.

Yours In Scouting,
Josh McWilliam
Pack 232 Committee Chair
membership@pack232.com

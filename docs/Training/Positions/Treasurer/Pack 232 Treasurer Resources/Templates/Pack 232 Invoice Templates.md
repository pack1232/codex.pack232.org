# Annual Dues
## New Scout Dues
### Invoice Email

Subject: Cub Scout Pack 232 Annual Dues

Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 232!
Our membership coordinator should be reaching out to you shortly to help you get started with the Pack.

I am the Pack 232 Treasurer and. I am reaching out to settle up your annual registration fees.  This fee is a straight pass through to the Twin Rivers Council to help fund the services and insurance they and the national organization provide.

The annual registration fee for existing scouts is $70. The Pack subsidizes new scout registrations as this is less than what council actually charges us. The optional Scout Life Magazine subscription is an additional $15.

If you already paid council while registering through them then I have credited your invoice for that amount.  They only change through the end of the calendar year while the amount we collect will cover you for the full school year.

If I have calculated this in error or you have any questions please feel free to reach out to me by replying to this email.

Thank you,
Josh McWilliam
Treasurer
Cub Scout Pack 232
money@pack232.com

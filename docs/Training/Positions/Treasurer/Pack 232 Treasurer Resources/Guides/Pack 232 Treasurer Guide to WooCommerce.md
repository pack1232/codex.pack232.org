# Overview
WooCommerce is the most popular ecommerce Wordpress (which our pack232.com site is built with) plugin and we used it in 2023 to enable proactive payments from families.

The idea was that rather than having to issue a bill to them and then have them pay it, they could simply go to the website, select the thing they wanted to pay for and checkout. With Wordpress’s integration into Quickbooks Online the idea was that those payments would then be automatically imported into our system to further make the Treasurer’s job easier.

We stopped doing this in later 2023 when we stopped accepting online payments, but we should reconsider adding this again in the future.

## Accessing WooCommerce
This must be done via our website’s admin area accessible here:

https://www.pack232.com/wp-admin

# Managing WooCommerce
## Transaction Log
This shows all the payments and refunds issued.

https://www.pack232.com/wp-admin/admin.php?page=wc-admin&path=%2Fpayments%2Ftransactions

## Issuing Refunds

IMPORTANT NOTE: WooCommerce does not reimburse the fees charged during the initial transaction so we end up losing money on a refunded transaction.

Distributing Funds
Payments accrue in our account until a distribution is requested.

Go to the WooCommerce > Payments > Payouts

https://www.pack232.com/wp-admin/admin.php?page=wc-admin&path=%2Fpayments%2Fpayouts

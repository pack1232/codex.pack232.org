# Overview
## Goals / Outcomes
The goals of reconciling our accounts are as follows:

Review transactions to identify incorrect or inconsistent transactions that might need correction or further investigation.
Make sure our transactions and final balance match that of the bank.
Make sure transactions are recorded correctly, including the appropriate category, vendor and/or customer, to aid with useful reporting later.
Download and save account statements to archive in Google Drive.
Enable up to date records in Quickbooks that will allow us to later run periodic reporting to report on financial status.
## Checklists
All of the details of how to do the tasks below are included further below in the document. This section is meant to serve as a quick reference for the period tasks that must be completed.
### Monthly Checklist
The following tasks should be completed monthly and are presented in logical order. “Prior to Meeting” means they should be completed prior to whenever the monthly Pack Committee Meeting is in order to enable the updated financial reports that should be provided for that meeting.

Note that as of the last update of this document in November of 2024 we primarily only use the Broadview accounts so reconciling the other accounts may be as simple as ensuring the account is properly synced and up to date in Quickbooks with nothing else needed if no transactions have occurred.
# Reconciling Accounts in Quickbooks
We use Quickbooks online to import all transactions from our bank accounts. One of the first steps each month is to go into Quickbooks and review the transactions that have occurred and make sure they match the record of the bank itself.
## Syncing Account Transactions in Quickbooks
This is done by first logging into Quickbooks Online. Our login is money@pack232.com. Once you are logged in, you will want to go to “Transactions” and then “Bank Transactions” (the first/default tab) to view the accounts that need to be reconciled.

If the accounts have not been synced with their respective institutions recently you will want to click the “Update” button in the upper right to force that and wait a minute for it to complete.

We have accounts with multiple institutions, each of which is documented further below, but first some general guidelines that apply to all accounts.
# General Reconciliation Guidelines
Quickbooks automatically imports all transactions from our accounts and automatically categorizes them based on past transactions so this generally isn’t very difficult.

Look for any transactions where Quickbooks automatically identified a match and click the “Match” button to enter them. These typically correspond to invoice or bills or invoices that have already been entered and paid.
In cases where it found multiple matches, this just means there are multiple transactions for the same amount. This happens most frequently related to parents making payments for something, which are typically the same amount for a given event. Try to select the best match. If there are no other defining characteristics of the transaction (ie. different vendor/customer indicated) simply select the transaction you entered that most closely matches the date of the bank transaction.

Here are a few tips to consider:

Reconciliation becomes much easier if you enter bills and invoices (charges to parents) in advance. This results in many transactions being matched automatically to the bill or invoice you already entered and the majority of the other recurring transactions are often common enough that Quickbooks can automatically categorize most. Membership Dues
If you enter a transaction into Quickbooks  as it happens, which often is a good idea, do not mark it as cleared at the time. Wait till the bank imports its record of it and then use the Bank Transactions interface in Quickbooks this guide discusses to match it to the transactions you entered. It will often do this automatically, but if you mark it cleared in advance, Quickbooks will not find a match as it only looks at not yet cleared transactions to do so, and you will have to either exclude that bank transaction or end up entering a duplicate. If you do end up clearing it in advance, I would go in and unclear it so you can then match it to the bank transaction imported (which then marks it as cleared).

# Position-Specific Courses

**Source:** [Scouting America](https://www.scouting.org/training/position-specific-courses/) | **PDF Reference:** [Trained Leader Requirements (PDF)](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/training/pdf/trainedleader_requirements.pdf)

Position-specific courses are designed to help Scouters learn how to effectively carry out their Scouting roles. In most cases, completing the course will make a Scouter "trained" for the role(s) covered in the course. These courses are available in Scouting America's Learn Center at [my.scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org). Youth Protection training is also required for all BSA registered volunteers.

Online training plans are organized into three phases: **Before 1st Meeting**, **First 30 Days**, and **Position Trained**. Classroom alternatives are also available for most programs.

# Position Trained Requirements

**Source:** [BSA Scouting.org](https://www.scouting.org/training/position-trained-requirements/) | **Updated:** July 2024

## Overview

A Trained Leader is a registered adult. Youth Protection Training is a joining requirement for all registered adults and must be retaken every two years (some councils require it to be retaken every year).

Adult leaders in units are considered "trained" and eligible to wear the official Trained emblem when they have completed Youth Protection Training and the training courses outlined below.

The full PDF document is available at: [Trained Leader Requirements (Updated July 2024)](https://www.scouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Position-Trained-Requirements-July2024.pdf)

Hello Everyone,

As we move into the 2025 popcorn selling season I realized I should follow up with something I should have done long ago and that is to inform everyone of the Scout Account funds earned for 2024 for those popcorn sellers that went above and beyond.

If you are receiving this email it is because your scout was among those who sold / received donations in excess of $600 (twice the minimum). The Pack decided it would share 15% of every sale / donation a scout received beyond that $600 number.

*(An image with the account balance totals was attached but could not be converted.)*

These funds may be used for any scouting event / activity such as camping trips, camporees, summer camps, etc. from last year or any future year. To use them all you need to do is send an email to treasurer@pack232.com and ask to use your scout account funds to pay an amount owed for an activity. If it is something you pay for directly (i.e. paying council for summer camp) then just submit a receipt and we will reimburse you from available funds in your Scout Account.

For those that are no longer with Pack 232, for those that have remained in scouting and moved on to another unit that also has a Scout Account program (i.e. Troop 75), we can transfer your balance to that unit.

Please let me know if you have any questions.

Thanks,
Josh

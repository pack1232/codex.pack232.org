# Training Awards and Recognition

**Source:** [Scouting America](https://www.scouting.org/training/training-awards-and-recognition/) | **Published:** 2024

## Content

Training awards and keys are designed to recognize unit Scouters for tenure, training, and performance in their leadership roles. These awards are available across all Scouting America programs including Cub Scouts, Scouts BSA, Venturing, and Sea Scouts.

Award knots are worn centered on the left side of the uniform shirt, positioned above the left pocket in rows of three. The bottom edge of knots should touch the top of the pocket flap. No more than 15 knots are typically recommended for wear simultaneously.

## Cub Scout Leader Awards

### Den Leader Training Award

**Tenure:** One year as registered Cub Scout Den Leader

**Training Requirements:**
- Complete Fast Start training for Den Leaders
- Complete Basic Training for Cub Scout Den Leaders
- Complete Youth Protection training
- Participate in Cub Scout Leader Pow Wow, University of Scouting, or attend minimum four roundtables

**Performance (five of the following):**
- Achieve 50% advancement to Wolf or Bear rank
- Reregister 75% of eligible members during pack rechartering
- Graduate 60% of members into Webelos Scouting
- Have assistant den leader or second adult present regularly
- Have den chief attending meetings regularly
- Lead den service project
- Conduct minimum three meetings monthly for nine months
- Participate in day camp or resident camp
- Explore three Ethics in Action activities
- Hold regular planning sessions with assistant

### Webelos Den Leader Award

**Tenure:** One year as registered Webelos den leader

**Training Requirements:**
- Complete Fast Start training for Webelos Den Leaders
- Complete Basic Training for Webelos den leaders
- Complete outdoor training for Webelos den leaders
- Complete Youth Protection training
- Participate in Cub Scout Leader Pow Wow, University of Scouting, or attend minimum four roundtables

**Performance (seven of the following):**
- Achieve 50% advancement in rank (Webelos badge or Arrow of Light)
- Reregister 75% during pack rechartering
- Graduate 60% into Scouts BSA
- Have assistant den leader or second adult attending regularly
- Have den chief attending regularly
- Lead two annual overnight campouts or outdoor activities
- Assist planning Webelos-Scouts BSA joint activity
- Lead den service project
- Conduct minimum three meetings per approved plan
- Participate in day camp or resident camp
- Explore three Ethics in Action activities
- Hold regular planning sessions with assistant

### Cub Scouter Award

**Tenure:** Two years as registered adult leader in Cub Scout pack

**Training Requirements:**
- Complete Fast Start training for position
- Complete basic training for Cub Scouting position
- Complete Youth Protection training
- Participate in Cub Scout Leader Pow Wow or University of Scouting annually, or attend minimum four roundtables per year

**Performance (five of the following):**
- Assist planning pack program achieving 50% advancement in rank
- Serve with Quality Unit Award-earning pack
- Lead pack activity (Blue and Gold dinner, pinewood derby, etc.)
- Lead promotional effort for Scouting magazine subscriptions (60% participation)
- Develop or update den activity book for 12+ Cub Scout themes
- Lead pack service project
- Organize participation in Cub Scout activities
- Support two annual Friends of Scouting campaigns
- Serve as leader for camp attendance
- Serve one year as pack trainer with 75% leader training completion

### Cubmaster Award

**Tenure:** One year as assistant Cubmaster and one year as Cubmaster, OR two years as Cubmaster

**Training Requirements:**
- Complete Fast Start training for Cubmasters
- Complete Basic Training for Cubmasters
- Complete Youth Protection training
- Participate in Cub Scout Leader Pow Wow, University of Scouting, or attend minimum four roundtables annually

**Performance:**
- Earn national Quality Unit Award minimum twice
- Earn National Summertime Pack Award minimum once
- Plan and conduct pack meetings each year registered

## Scouts BSA Leader Awards

### Scouts BSA Leader's Training Award

**Tenure:** Two years as registered adult Scouts BSA leader

**Training Requirements:**
- Complete Scouts BSA Leader Fast Start training
- Complete Scoutmaster Fundamentals (three-part basic training)
- Complete Youth Protection training

**Performance (five of the following):**
- Participate in support role for five overnight campouts
- Help with two annual unit or district Friends of Scouting enrollments
- Serve on staff of council or district training event
- Participate in supplemental training course
- Participate actively in three troop parents' nights or courts of honor
- Supervise troop money-earning project
- Serve as merit badge counselor for minimum five Scouts
- Successfully complete Wood Badge
- Fulfill troop committee function per guidelines
- Actively assist Webelos den for six months
- Participate in six roundtables
- Help organize or reorganize Scout troop

### Scoutmaster Award of Merit

**Tenure:** Minimum 18 months service as Scoutmaster

**Requirements:**
- Completion of Scoutmaster Fundamentals (basic training)
- Achievement of Quality Unit Award minimum once
- Successful training of youth troop leaders
- Senior Patrol Leader and Troop Committee Chair recommend Scoutmaster based on unit performance over past 18 months

### Scoutmaster's Key

**Training:**
- Complete Scouts BSA Fast Start Training
- Complete three-part Scoutmaster Fundamentals: "The Troop Meeting," "Troop Organization," "The Outdoor Program"
- Conduct troop junior leader training three times
- Participate as adult in junior leader training
- Participate in one supplemental training course beyond basics
- Earn Scouts BSA Leader's Training Award

**Roundtable Attendance:** Minimum six roundtables annually for three years

**Tenure:** Minimum three years as registered Scoutmaster within five-year period

**Performance:** Earn Scouts BSA Troop National Quality Unit Award twice

## Scouter's Training Award

The Scouter's Training Award can be earned by Cub Scout, Scouts BSA, Venturing, and Sea Scout leaders as well as District Committee members. The award can be earned more than once and in multiple program areas.

**Insignia:** Green knot on tan background, worn over the left pocket, with program-specific device additions for Cub Scouts, Scouts BSA, Venturing, and Sea Scouts. A medal and certificate are also available.

### General Requirements

- Minimum two years of tenure as a registered adult leader in the program
- Complete "This Is Scouting" training
- Complete basic training for position held
- Attend a Pow Wow, University of Scouting, or minimum four roundtable meetings during each year of tenure
- Participate in annual planning meeting each year
- Unit must achieve at least Bronze level of Journey to Excellence
- Give primary leadership in meeting at least one unit Journey to Excellence objective
- Participate in at least one additional supplemental or advanced training event at the council level or higher

Progress Record and Application forms are available at [scouting.org/training/adult](https://www.scouting.org/training/adult/). Once requirements are met, the completed document should be taken to the local Council Service Center for processing.

## Scouter's Key

The Scouter's Key is a more advanced recognition requiring additional tenure, training, and performance beyond the Scouter's Training Award.

**Insignia:** Fleur-de-lis superimposed over a key suspended from green ribbon worn above left pocket; or a green and white knot on tan background, worn above left pocket.

**General Requirements:**
- Minimum three years of registered tenure in position within a five-year period
- Completion of the Scouter's Training Award for the applicable program
- Additional training and performance requirements specific to position

## Commissioner Awards

### Unit Commissioner Key

**Training:**
- Complete Commissioner Basic Training sessions: "Why Commissioners?," "Units: The Commissioner's Greatest Priority," "How to Help a Unit"
- Complete personal coaching orientation including orientation projects

**Tenure:** Three years as registered commissioner within five-year period

**Performance (all required):**
- Fill in and follow up on Commissioner Work Sheets for each assigned unit
- Conduct membership and leadership inventories in assigned units
- Attend six district commissioner staff meetings
- Provide training topic for one staff meeting
- Participate in charter review meeting resulting in on-time unit preregistration
- Participate in charter presentation
- Attend council commissioner conference or actively participate in major council event
- Earn Arrowhead Honor Award for position

### Roundtable Staff Training Award

**Tenure:** Two years as roundtable staff member

**Training:**
- Review orientation material in current roundtable guides
- Review all material in current program helps
- Complete Fast Start and basic training for roundtable commissioners and staff

**Performance:**
- Participate in six roundtable staff meetings
- Actively assist in six roundtables
- Conduct successful attendance promotion project
- Develop and exhibit display related to theme at one roundtable
- Conduct preopening activity and opening ceremony
- Conduct or be responsible for major project, presentation, or demonstration at one roundtable

### Arrowhead Honor Award

Available for District Commissioners, Assistant District Commissioners, Roundtable Commissioners, and Unit Commissioners. Requirements are specific to each position and generally include completing basic training, attending staff meetings, participating in charter processes, and attending council commissioner conferences.

### Distinguished Commissioner Service Award

**Requirements:**
- Complete local council training including earning Scouter's Key for Commissioners
- Serve as active commissioner or Exploring service team member for five consecutive years; currently registered
- Recharge minimum 90% of assigned units for minimum two consecutive years
- Achieve National Quality Unit Award with more than 50% of units in service area for minimum two consecutive years

## District Committee Awards

### District Committee Key

**Training:**
- Review "The District" and position highlight book
- Discuss district role with committee chairman or district executive
- Complete District Committee Training Workshop

**Tenure:** Three years as district committee member within five-year period

**Performance:**
- District earns Quality District Award minimum once within three years
- Actively participate in six district committee meetings
- Lead eight projects of operating committee or approved projects (unit organization, training course, camporee, Friends of Scouting, etc.)

---

## Outstanding Service Awards

### District Award of Merit
Awarded by District for service to youth, typically exceeding five years of service. Self-nomination disqualifies candidate.

### Silver Beaver Award
Presented by Council Executive Board for outstanding service to youth within council. Average tenure is ten years or longer. Self-nomination disqualifies candidate.

### Silver Antelope Award
Presented by Regional Executive Board for service to youth within region. Self-nomination disqualifies candidate.

### Silver Buffalo Award
Awarded by National Executive Board for outstanding service to youth on national basis. Self-nomination disqualifies candidate.

---

## Other Recognition Awards

### James E. West Fellowship Award
Recognition for individuals and chartered units contributing $1,000 or more in cash or securities to council endowment fund. Payment may be made over four-year period.

### Whitney Young, Jr. Award
Recognizes outstanding support reaching under-served rural and inner-city areas.

### William T. Hornaday Awards
Recognize significant contributions to conservation, established in 1917.

### International Scouter's Award
Encourages Scouters to participate in world Scouting activities. Requires completion of six items from minimum three categories of international Scouting involvement.

### George Meany Award
National recognition for union member adults making significant youth community contributions through Scouting.

**Important:** All dates of service used to earn one key or award cannot be used to earn another key or award.

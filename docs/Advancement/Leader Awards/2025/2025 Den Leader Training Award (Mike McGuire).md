# Awarded to Michael McGuire on 06/2/2025
[call up the award recipient so he is front and center...]

I would like to call Mike McGuire to the stage.

[once the recipient is on stage, introduce the award….]

The Den Leader Training Award recognizes a leader who has gone above and beyond to provide an exceptional experience for their Scouts. Through completing required and advanced official training, leading a successful den for at least a full year, and actively participating in events, planning, and service projects, this award honors a commitment to Scouting’s core values. It reflects a leader who not only shows up—but shows up prepared, engaged, and dedicated to helping every Scout grow.

[present the award….]

On behalf of Scouting America, it is my pleasure to present the Den Leader Training Award to Michael McGuire.

Thank you Mike.

[present award, shake hands, make sure someone gets a picture]

# Awarded to Richard Crumb on 06/2/2025
[call up the award recipient so he is front and center...]

I would like to call Richard Crumb to the stage.

[once the recipient is on stage, introduce the award….]

The Scouter’s Training Award recognizes a dedicated leader who has shown a strong commitment to the Cub Scout program through consistent service, training, and leadership. Over the course of at least two years, this leader has not only completed advanced training but has also helped the pack plan meaningful programs and achieve excellence. This award honors their reliability, initiative, and the important role they’ve played in making Scouting a great experience for our youth.

[present the award….]

On behalf of Scouting America, it is my pleasure to present the Scouter’s Training Award for Cub Scout Leaders to Richard Crumb.

Thank you Richard.

[present award, shake hands, make sure someone gets a picture]

Note: This particular knot is being retired and so Richard will be the last of us to receive it.

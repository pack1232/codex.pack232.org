# Awarded to Richard Crumb on 06/2/2025
[call up the award recipient so he is front and center...]

I would like to call Richard Crumb to the stage.

[once the recipient is on stage, introduce the award….]

The Cubmaster’s Key Award recognizes a leader who has shown exceptional commitment, training, and performance over several years of service. It reflects a deep dedication to building a strong Cub Scout program, including planning quality activities, completing advanced training, and guiding the pack to meet key goals. This award is a symbol of consistent, outstanding leadership that has made a lasting impact on Scouts and families alike.

[present the award….]

On behalf of Scouting America, it is my pleasure to present the Cubmaster’s Key Award to Richard Crumb for his years of dedicated service as Cubmaster of our Pack.

Thank you Richard.

[present award, shake hands, make sure someone gets a picture]

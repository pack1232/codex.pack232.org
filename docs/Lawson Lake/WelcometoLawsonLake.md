Welcome to Lawson Lake!
A few things to help you during your stay:
- The heat for the front room is on the back wall near the firewood. The fan control is next to the door as you come in.
- Feel free to use the wood.  It is great and dry. Please bring more pre-chopped wood in from the fire circle before you leave.
- Camp is yours for the time you are here! Feel free to explore, use what you would like, however, please remember to use “Leave No Trace” principals.
- If there is an emergency 911 call button at the lake front on the boat shed if you need.
- The master lock key opens cabin 55 and the nurse’s station. Both have a phone that can dial locally
- Internet password is 5187682560
- The keys are located in the scramble lock next to the Waldman lodge door.  The code is 2112.  The house key opens up the lodge.
A few things before you leave:
- Please turn off the heat/fans and make sure the fire is out in the fireplace.
- Please replace the wood you used with wood from the pile near the fire circle.
- Bring your trash to the dumpster in the parking lot on your way out. Leave the recycling, I will bring it home with me to recycle, or you are welcome to take it home to recycle.
- Please turn off all lights and lock the door.
- Place the keys back into the combo lock and scramble the numbers. Combo to the lock is 2112
- If anything is broken, or in need of repair, please let us know.

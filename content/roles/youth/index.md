# Youth Roles

Youth leadership positions in a Scouts BSA troop give Scouts the opportunity to develop real-world leadership skills. These positions fall into two categories: **elected** roles chosen by the Scouts themselves, and **appointed** roles assigned by the Senior Patrol Leader with Scoutmaster approval.

## Elected Positions

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| [Senior Patrol Leader](./senior-patrol-leader/) | Top elected youth leader; runs troop meetings and leads the Patrol Leaders' Council |
| [Assistant Senior Patrol Leader](./assistant-senior-patrol-leader/) | Fills in for the SPL; trains new patrol leaders and coordinates appointed leaders |
| [Patrol Leader](./patrol-leader/) | Elected leader of a patrol; represents the patrol on the PLC |

## Appointed Positions

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| [Troop Guide](./troop-guide/) | Mentors the new-Scout patrol; helps new Scouts learn skills and earn ranks |
| [Quartermaster](./quartermaster/) | Manages troop equipment and supplies; maintains gear inventory |
| [Scribe](./scribe/) | Records attendance and maintains troop records |
| [Instructor](./instructor/) | Teaches Scouting skills to other troop members |
| [Chaplain Aide](./chaplain-aide/) | Assists the troop chaplain; leads prayers and promotes Scout reverence |
| [Historian](./historian/) | Documents troop history through photos, records, and scrapbooks |
| [Librarian](./librarian/) | Manages the troop library of merit badge books, handbooks, and magazines |
| [Outdoor Ethics Guide](./outdoor-ethics-guide/) | Promotes Leave No Trace principles and outdoor ethics |
| [Junior Assistant Scoutmaster](./junior-assistant-scoutmaster/) | Ages 16-17; serves as a bridge between youth and adult leaders |
| [Bugler](./bugler/) | Sounds bugle calls for troop ceremonies and campouts |
| [Order of the Arrow Representative](./order-of-the-arrow-rep/) | Liaison between the troop and the OA lodge |

## Shared Positions

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| [Den Chief](../shared/den-chief/) | Scouts BSA youth who assists a Cub Scout den |

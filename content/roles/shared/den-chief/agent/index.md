# Den Chief Agent

## Agent Purpose

The Den Chief agent is designed to help den chiefs prepare for den meetings, understand their role, and maximize their impact with Cub Scouts.

---

## Available Skills

*(To be populated as skills are built)*

- Den meeting activity planner (from the den chief's perspective)
- Den chief role explainer
- Recognition and leadership tips

---

## Trigger Scenarios

Invoke this agent when you need help with:

- Understanding what to do at your first den meeting
- Planning a gathering activity or game to lead
- Preparing for a joint den-troop activity
- Drafting a report of your den chief service (for Scouts BSA advancement)

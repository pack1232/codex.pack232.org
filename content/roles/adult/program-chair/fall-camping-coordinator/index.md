# Fall Camping Coordinator

## Role Summary

The Fall Camping Coordinator plans the pack's fall camping trip — often a "Fall Back to Scouting" event that doubles as a fun kickoff to the new program year and a recruitment opportunity.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Program Chair |
| Works with | Cubmaster, Grubmaster (Assistant Cubmaster), Member Recruitment Coordinator |

## Core Responsibilities

- Select and reserve a campsite or event venue for the fall outing
- Plan the event program in coordination with the Cubmaster
- Coordinate food planning with the Grubmaster
- Ensure at least one BALOO-trained adult is attending (if overnight)
- Promote the event as a recruitment opportunity — invite prospective families
- Communicate event details to families: dates, cost, packing list, logistics
- Collect registration fees (coordinate with Treasurer)
- Manage headcount and confirm attendance
- Ensure health forms and consent forms are collected for all participants

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| June–July | Select and reserve campsite/venue |
| August–September | Begin family communication; open registration |
| September | Confirm headcount; finalize plans |
| October | Execute fall camping trip / Fall Back to Scouting |

# Pinewood Derby Coordinator

## Role Summary

The Pinewood Derby Coordinator plans and executes the pack's annual Pinewood Derby — one of the most popular and well-attended events of the Cub Scout year. This includes venue logistics, race track setup, registration, race management, and awards.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Program Chair |
| Works with | Cubmaster, Advancement Chair, Treasurer |

## Core Responsibilities

- Coordinate the annual Pinewood Derby event from planning through execution
- Secure the venue (typically your pack meeting location gym) and confirm the date
- Arrange for the race track: setup, testing, and takedown
- Manage car registration and weigh-in process
- Recruit volunteers for race day roles (judges, registration, track managers)
- Coordinate with the Advancement Chair on trophies/awards
- Communicate event details to families: date, rules, car specs, schedule
- Ensure BSA Pinewood Derby rules are followed
- Coordinate press coverage with the Public Relations Coordinator

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| January | Confirm date and venue; distribute car kits |
| February | Host optional workshop day for car building |
| March | Execute Pinewood Derby event (typically March) |
| After event | Submit results; coordinate press release |

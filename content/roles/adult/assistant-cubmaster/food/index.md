# Assistant Cubmaster for Food

## Role Summary

The Assistant Cubmaster for Food (a.k.a. Grubmaster) manages food provisioning for pack campouts and outdoor events — menu planning, grocery purchasing, food inventory, and meal preparation coordination. Good food makes a campout great.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Cubmaster |
| Works with | Camping Coordinators (Program Chair), Treasurer, Equipment Coordinator |

## Core Responsibilities

- Plan menus for campouts and outdoor events (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks)
- Account for dietary restrictions, allergies, and preferences (gather info before each event)
- Create shopping lists and purchase groceries (coordinate budget with Treasurer)
- Manage food inventory and storage during campouts
- Coordinate cooking assignments among attending adults
- Ensure food safety: proper storage, handling, and cooking temperatures
- Plan for age-appropriate Scout involvement in cooking (Scouts love to help!)
- Manage cleanup and waste disposal after meals
- Submit receipts to Treasurer for reimbursement

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| Before each campout | Plan menu, gather dietary info, create shopping list |
| 1 week before | Purchase groceries and supplies |
| During campout | Coordinate meals, cooking, and cleanup |
| After campout | Submit receipts; note what worked and what to adjust |

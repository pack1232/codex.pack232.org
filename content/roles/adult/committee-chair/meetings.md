# Committee Chair: Meetings

## Pack Committee Meeting

The monthly pack committee meeting is the operational heartbeat of the pack. The Committee Chair calls and presides over this meeting.

**Cadence**: Monthly (same week as the pack meeting, or the week before/after)
**Attendees**: All committee members, Cubmaster, Assistant Cubmaster, den leaders encouraged

### Suggested Agenda Template

1. **Opening / Call to Order** — Committee Chair
2. **Approval of prior meeting minutes** — Secretary
3. **Treasurer's Report** — Treasurer (financial status, budget vs. actual)
4. **Cubmaster's Report** — Program update, upcoming pack meeting plans
5. **Committee Reports** — Brief updates from each chair (Advancement, Activities, Membership, Popcorn, Marketing, Service)
6. **Old Business** — Follow-ups from prior meeting
7. **New Business** — New items, decisions needed
8. **Upcoming Events Review** — Confirm next month's schedule
9. **Adjournment**

### Tips

- Keep each report to 2–3 minutes unless a decision is needed
- Assign action items explicitly with an owner and due date
- The Secretary records minutes — only key items, decisions, and action items (not a verbatim transcript)
- Send the agenda to all attendees 2–3 days before the meeting

## Pack Leaders' Meeting

The Cubmaster chairs the pack leaders' meeting (sometimes combined with the committee meeting). This is focused on program coordination rather than administration.

**Attendees**: Cubmaster, Assistant Cubmaster, all Den Leaders, Assistant Den Leaders

**Purpose**:
- Coordinate den participation at the upcoming pack meeting
- Share program updates and BSA information
- Resolve issues between dens
- Update den leaders on advancement, schedule, and events

---

## Annual Pack Program Planning Conference

Held once a year (typically May–August), this meeting sets the pack's program calendar for the upcoming Scouting year.

**Led by**: Cubmaster and Committee Chair jointly
**Attendees**: Cubmaster, Assistant Cubmaster, all Den Leaders, Committee Chair, key committee members

**Outputs**:
- Approved pack meeting calendar (dates, themes)
- Major events calendar (Blue & Gold, Pinewood Derby, camping, hikes, recruitment)
- Activity coordinators assigned to events
- Draft budget inputs

# Redemptions Coordinator

## Role Summary

The Redemptions Coordinator (a.k.a. Can Captain) manages the pack's bottle and can redemption program — a low-effort, ongoing fundraiser where families save returnable bottles and cans and bring them to a designated collection point. Proceeds go to the pack's general fund or Scout accounts.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Fundraising Chair |
| Works with | Treasurer, Committee Chair |

## Core Responsibilities

- Manage the bottle and can collection shed/bin (location, maintenance, signage)
- Coordinate regular redemption runs to turn in collected bottles and cans
- Track redemption proceeds and report totals to the Treasurer
- Promote the program to pack families — remind them to save returnables
- Ensure the collection area stays clean and organized
- Coordinate with the Fundraising Chair on whether proceeds go to the general fund, Scout accounts, or a specific purpose

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| Year-round | Collect and redeem bottles/cans on a regular schedule |
| Monthly | Report proceeds to Treasurer |
| September | Remind families about the program at the start of the new year |

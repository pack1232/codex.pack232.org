# Secretary Agent

## Agent Purpose

The Secretary agent is designed to help with meeting minutes drafting, communications, records management, and administrative correspondence.

---
audience: adult

## Available Skills

*(To be populated as skills are built)*

- Meeting minutes formatter
- Committee meeting notice drafter
- YPT expiration tracker
- Family email composer

---

## Trigger Scenarios

Invoke this agent when you need help with:

- Formatting and editing committee meeting minutes
- Drafting a committee meeting notice/agenda email
- Writing a family announcement (pack meeting reminder, event info)
- Tracking which adults need YPT renewal
- Drafting a thank-you letter or correspondence

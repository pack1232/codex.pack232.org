# Den Leader Guide: Bears (3rd Grade)

## Overview

Bears is the Cub Scouting program for third graders. Bears are growing in independence and confidence. Meetings typically happen twice a month. The den structure uses a den leader, assistant den leader, and den chief.

## Meeting Structure

1. **Before the Meeting** — Den leader, assistant, and den chief make final preparations
2. **Gathering** — Scouts arrive and join a game or activity; den leader takes attendance and checks requirement completion
3. **Opening** — Scout Oath, Scout Law, Pledge of Allegiance
4. **Activities** — Scouts complete activities for the current adventure requirements
5. **Closing** — Reminders, closing thought, brief inspirational ceremony; denner leads cleanup
6. **After the Meeting** — Den leader, assistant, and den chief discuss next meeting

## Tips for New Bear Den Leaders

- Third graders can handle more complex, multi-step activities than younger ranks — take advantage of this
- The denner role (Scout youth leader within the den) becomes more meaningful at Bear level; assign it and rotate it
- Bears are a great age for service projects — good to start building that habit
- Keep parent involvement active even though it's not required; parents who are engaged at Bear level stay through Arrow of Light

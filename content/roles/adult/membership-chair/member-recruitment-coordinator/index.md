# Member Recruitment Coordinator

## Role Summary

The Member Recruitment Coordinator drives new member recruitment events and outreach for the pack. This includes planning and executing school-night sign-ups, activities fairs, and year-round recruitment efforts to grow the pack's membership.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Membership Chair |
| Works with | Cubmaster, Public Relations Coordinator, Den Leaders |

## Core Responsibilities

- Plan and execute the annual school-night recruitment event at your pack meeting location
- Staff a the pack booth at community activities fairs (e.g., your town Activities Fair)
- Prepare recruitment materials: fliers, sign-up sheets, info packets, BSA brochures
- Recruit current Scouts and families to attend recruitment events in uniform
- Follow up with every interested family within 48 hours of a recruitment event
- Coordinate with the Public Relations Coordinator on social media and flier promotion
- Track recruitment outcomes: contacts made, sign-ups, conversions to registered Scouts
- Coordinate with Cubmaster on year-round recruitment messaging
- Follow up with former pack members now in a troop as potential den chiefs

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| August | Prepare recruitment materials; attend community activities fairs |
| September | Host school-night recruitment event(s); follow up with prospects |
| Year-round | Respond to inquiries; welcome walk-in interest |

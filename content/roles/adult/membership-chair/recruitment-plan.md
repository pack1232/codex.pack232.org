# Membership Chair: Recruitment Plan

## Overview

The primary recruitment season for Cub Scouting is September, timed to school start. However, Scouts can join at any time of year. A year-round recruitment mindset keeps the pack healthy.

---

## Annual Recruitment Timeline

| Month | Activity |
|---|---|
| August | Attend community activities fairs (e.g., your town Activities Fair) with pack booth |
| Late August | Prepare recruitment materials: fliers, sign-up forms, promotional items |
| August–September | Coordinate with Public Relations Coordinator for school communications and social media |
| September | Host school-night recruitment event(s) at your pack meeting location |
| September | Follow up with interested families within 48 hours of sign-up |
| October–November | New family orientation for families who joined in September |
| Year-round | Welcome new Scouts who join at any time; connect to den leader |
| Year-round | Follow up on dropouts and lapsed members |

---

## School-Night Recruitment Event

The school-night sign-up is the most important recruitment event of the year.

**Planning steps:**
1. Coordinate with your pack meeting location for permission to use school grounds or have fliers distributed
2. Book the date (BSA often provides district-wide school-night dates — coordinate with your council)
3. Prepare sign-up table: promotional materials, applications, info sheets, tablet/laptop for digital sign-ups
4. Recruit current pack families to bring their Scouts in uniform — uniformed Scouts are the best advertisement
5. Coordinate with Cubmaster to be present and speak to prospective families
6. Follow up: email or call every interested family within 48 hours

**At the event:**
- Greet every family warmly — this is their first impression of the pack
- Have sign-up materials ready and registration fees clearly communicated
- Give every interested family an info packet with pack contact info and website

---

## Community Activities Fairs

the pack participates in community activities fairs (e.g., the your town Activities Fair) as a recruitment tool.

- Set up a booth with pack branding, sign-up sheets, and Cub Scout materials
- Scouts in uniform attending are the best recruitment tool
- Collect contact information; follow up after the fair

See: `docs/Training/Positions/Marketing - Public Relations Chair/Events/2025 your town Activities Fair.md`

---

## Year-Round Recruitment

Scouts can join at any time. When a new Scout joins mid-year:
- Connect them to their den leader immediately
- Assign them a "buddy" Scout in the den
- Ensure health form and adult application (if applicable) are submitted
- Membership Chair or new family coordinator conducts a brief welcome orientation

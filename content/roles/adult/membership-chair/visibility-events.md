# Public Relations: Visibility Events

## Overview

Community visibility events are opportunities to show the pack's presence and recruit new Scouts. The Public Relations Coordinator identifies, coordinates, and supports pack participation in these events.

---

## Key Visibility Events

### your town Activities Fair

An annual activities fair where community organizations — including the pack — set up booths to connect with families.

- See: `docs/Training/Positions/Marketing - Public Relations Chair/Events/2025 your town Activities Fair.md`
- **When**: Typically in late August / early September (timing to be confirmed annually)
- **Goal**: Sign up interested Scouts; collect contact info for follow-up
- **What to bring**: the pack table cover/banner, sign-up sheets, recruitment brochures, BSA promotional materials, pack info sheets, tablet for digital sign-ups, uniformed Scouts (best advertisement)
- **Follow up**: Share contact list with Membership Chair within 24 hours of event

### Community Parades and Events

the pack may march in or participate in community events (parades, school events). The Public Relations Coordinator:
- Identifies opportunities and brings them to the Committee Chair
- Coordinates Scout and leader participation
- Ensures pack is represented professionally (uniforms, signs)

### School Night for Scouting

While the Membership Chair leads school-night recruitment events, the Public Relations Coordinator supports by:
- Preparing fliers and promotional materials
- Ensuring Facebook event is posted
- Bringing branded materials (banner, pack info) to the event

---

## Visibility Event Checklist

- [ ] Date confirmed; any registration or booth fee paid (coordinate with Treasurer)
- [ ] Table/booth location confirmed
- [ ] Pack banner/tablecloth, promotional materials prepared
- [ ] Sign-up sheets or digital intake form ready
- [ ] Uniformed Scouts and leaders recruited to attend
- [ ] Post on Facebook before and after the event
- [ ] Contact list shared with Membership Chair after event

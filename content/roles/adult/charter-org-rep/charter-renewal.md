# Charter Org Rep: Charter Renewal

## Overview

Annual charter renewal is the process through which the pack re-establishes its official sanction from your council and its charter relationship with your charter organization. The Charter Org Rep plays a key role in signing the recharter documents and adult applications.

---

## COR Role in Charter Renewal

The Committee Chair drives the recharter process (see `../committee-chair/rechartering.md`). The COR's specific responsibilities are:

1. **Sign all adult applications** — Every adult registered with the pack must have their application signed by the COR. This includes both new applications and any renewals that require signature.

2. **Review and sign the recharter application** — Once the Committee Chair has prepared the recharter roster and document, the COR reviews and signs it before submission to your council.

3. **Represent the charter organization** — The COR must confirm that the pack's program continues to align with your charter organization's mission and values, and that the PTA is willing to continue the charter relationship.

4. **Charter presentation ceremony** — The COR typically participates in the annual charter presentation ceremony at a pack meeting (usually January or February), where the charter is formally renewed with the pack.

---

## Timeline

| Month | COR Task |
|---|---|
| October–November | Review draft roster with Committee Chair; sign adult applications for new leaders |
| November–December | Sign completed recharter application |
| December | Submit to your council (with Committee Chair) |
| January–February | Participate in charter presentation ceremony at pack meeting |

---

## Adult Application Signing

The COR signs all BSA adult applications before they are submitted to Council. This applies to:
- All new adult leaders
- Any re-registration requiring a new application

**Process**: Secretary collects completed applications and YPT certificates → Committee Chair reviews → COR signs → Secretary submits to Council

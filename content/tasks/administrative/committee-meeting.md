# Committee Meeting

Monthly pack committee meeting. Committee Chair presides. Includes treasurer's report, Cubmaster's report, committee member updates, old and new business, upcoming event review.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: Same week as or adjacent to the pack meeting
- **Months**: Every month

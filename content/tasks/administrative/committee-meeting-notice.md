# Committee Meeting Notice & Agenda

Secretary sends committee meeting notice with date, time, location, and agenda to all committee members and den leaders.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: 2–3 days before the committee meeting
- **Months**: Every month

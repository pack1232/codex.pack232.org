# Scout Rank Transitions in Scoutbook

Den leaders formally transition Scouts to the next rank (Wolves → Bears, etc.) in Scoutbook. Cubmaster coordinates crossover between den leaders.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: June–July: update Scoutbook as Scouts move to the next rank's den
- **Months**: June, July

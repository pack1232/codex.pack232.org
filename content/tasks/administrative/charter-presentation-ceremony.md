# Charter Presentation Ceremony

Formal ceremony at the pack meeting in which the new charter is presented to the pack by the Charter Org Rep, renewing the pack's sanction for the new year.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: January pack meeting — formal renewal of the pack's charter
- **Months**: January

# Recharter Submission to Council

Committee Chair submits completed recharter to your council. Charter Org Rep signs. Treasurer pays recharter fees.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: December deadline — confirm exact date with your council each year
- **Months**: December

# Recharter: Collect & Route Adult Applications

Secretary collects adult applications (with YPT certificates) for any new or previously unregistered adults. Routes to Charter Org Rep for signature.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: October–November — concurrent with roster review
- **Months**: October, November

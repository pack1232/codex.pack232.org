# Leader Roster Review

Committee Chair reviews the full leader roster to identify vacant positions, roles at risk of turnover, and dens that will need new leadership for the coming year.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Early July to identify gaps before recruitment season
- **Months**: July

# AOL Crossover: Confirm Troops & Ceremony Format

Contact and confirm attendance of local Scouts BSA troop Scoutmaster(s) and youth leaders for the crossover ceremony. Discuss ceremony format, Scout transitions, and any special traditions (bridge crossing, sash transfer, etc.). Confirm date and time. Send them advance details and Scout names.

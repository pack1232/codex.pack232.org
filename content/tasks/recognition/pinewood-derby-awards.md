# Pinewood Derby: Trophies & Style Awards

Order trophies for first, second, third place overall and by den. Order medals or ribbons for style awards (Most Realistic, Fastest Looking, Best Car That's Not a Car, Most Colorful, Most Humorous, Most Creative, Best In Show). Coordinate with Pinewood Derby Coordinator on quantity and delivery date.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Order by February to ensure delivery for March race
- **Months**: January, February

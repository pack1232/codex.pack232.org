# Arrow of Light Crossover Ceremony

Scouts who have earned the Arrow of Light badge cross over to a Scouts BSA troop. Ceremony involves Scoutmaster and troop youth leaders. Advancement Chair orders AOL badges and certificates well in advance.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: January target (February latest); at a pack meeting
- **Months**: January, February

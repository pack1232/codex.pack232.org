# Monthly Advancement Processing

Advancement Chair pulls completed advancement data from Scoutbook, purchases badges and awards from the Scout Shop, and prepares awards for presentation at the pack meeting. Submits advancement reports to your council.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: Before each pack meeting — awards must be in hand before the meeting
- **Months**: Every month

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [Advancement Chair](/roles/advancement-chair/) | Owner |
| [Den Leaders](/roles/den-leader/) | Involved |

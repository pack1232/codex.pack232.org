# Scouting for Food

Annual BSA food drive. Scouts collect canned goods door-to-door in assigned areas. Coordinate area assignments with district/other packs, arrange food pantry drop-off, provide bags and door hangers. Lead coordinator: Kara Giglia.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Typically November — coordinate with district for area assignment
- **Months**: November

# Memorial Day Parade

Lead the pack's participation in the your town Memorial Day Parade. Coordinate scout lineup at Herber & Winding at 10:40am, step off 11:00am. Class A uniforms. Ensure Pack Flag, American Flag, and Pack banner are carried. Wait with scouts at Veterans Memorial Park until all parents collect.

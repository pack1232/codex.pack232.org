# BSA Unit Money-Earning Application

Approval from both the charter organization and your council is required before any fundraising activity other than the annual popcorn sale. Fundraising Chair prepares the application; Charter Org Rep and Council must approve before any activity begins.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: as-needed
- **When**: Required before any non-popcorn fundraising activity begins
- **Months**: Every month

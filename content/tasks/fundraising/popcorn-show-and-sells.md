# Popcorn Show & Sells

Scouts staff tables in front of local merchants (grocery stores, etc.) and sell popcorn directly to customers. Kernel schedules shifts and coordinates inventory.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Multiple weekends in September–October; Kernel schedules with merchants
- **Months**: September, October

# Popcorn Pickup & Distribution

Popcorn Kernel picks up the pack's popcorn from the district, sorts it by Scout, and distributes to Scout families at a pack meeting or designated pickup.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: November — after council distributes popcorn to district
- **Months**: November

# Popcorn Order Submission to Council

Popcorn Kernel collects all Scout take-order forms, enters the pack's order into the Trail's End / Pecatonica system, and submits to your council District Office by the annual deadline.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: By the council's deadline — check trcscouting.org annually
- **Months**: October

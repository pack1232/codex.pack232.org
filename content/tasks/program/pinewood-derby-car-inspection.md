# Pinewood Derby: Car Inspection & Weigh-In Setup

Set up official inspection station and scales. Coordinate inspection committee volunteers. Prepare inspection checklist and violation forms. Verify that all cars meet the pack rules (dimensions, weight, wheel base, etc.). Notify families of inspection times and process.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: March race week — before race day
- **Months**: March

# Pack Meeting

Monthly pack meeting. All dens and families attend. Approximately one hour. Includes flag ceremony, program, advancement recognition, and closing.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: Consistent day each month (e.g. 3rd Monday); set at annual planning conference
- **Months**: Every month

# Pinewood Derby: Kit Purchase & Distribution

Pinewood Derby Team purchases Pinewood Derby kits for all Scouts (and Lego Derby kits for Lions). Distributes kits at a pack meeting or den meetings. Lead: Jeff Middendorf. Team: Dave Coup, Gary Robbins, Jeff Dievendorf, Charles Giglia, Chris Orth.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Early March — 4–6 weeks before race day
- **Months**: March

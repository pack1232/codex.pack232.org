# Den Meetings

Den leaders conduct 1–2 den meetings per month. Den leaders update Scoutbook with completed advancement requirements promptly after each meeting.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: Lions: 1x/month. Tigers and above: typically 2x/month.
- **Months**: Every month

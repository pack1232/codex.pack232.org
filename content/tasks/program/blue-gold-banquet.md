# Blue & Gold Banquet

Annual Blue & Gold Banquet celebrating Cub Scouting's founding. Recognizes Scout accomplishments, honors leaders, includes advancement ceremonies and entertainment. Coordinators: Becky D'Anza, Andrea Dievendorf, Lindsay Knott, Kara Giglia.

# Annual Program Planning Conference

Annual meeting to set the pack's program calendar for the coming Scouting year. Sets pack meeting dates, themes, major events, and assigns activity coordinators.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: July or August; before the Scouting year begins
- **Months**: July

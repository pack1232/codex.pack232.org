# New Scout Mid-Year Onboarding

Scouts can join at any time. Membership Chair connects new families to their den leader, provides welcome packet, and ensures health form and registration are complete.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: as-needed
- **When**: As new Scouts join at any point during the year
- **Months**: Every month

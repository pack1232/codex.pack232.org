# National Night Out Recruitment Booth

Staff the pack recruitment booth at your town Police Department's National Night Out community event. Setup at 4pm, event 5-8pm. Bring table, tent/canopy, chairs, recruitment materials, and pack banner. No electricity on-site. Rain or shine.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: First Tuesday of August — a local park, Delmar
- **Months**: August

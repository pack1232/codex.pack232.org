# USS Slater Overnight: Planning & Booking

Plan and book USS Slater overnight experience. Submit $100 non-refundable deposit early. Obtain proof of insurance listing USS SLATER/DEHM as certificate holder (due 3 weeks prior). Collect final payment ($50/person), participant roster, and medical forms. Capacity: 20–50 people.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: as-needed
- **When**: Book 3+ months in advance for spring overnight; biennial (every 2 years)
- **Months**: January, February

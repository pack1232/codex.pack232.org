# Summer Resident Camp Roster & Registration

Promote summer resident camp at your council camp to pack families. Manage pack roster in Scoutbook. Add registered scouts by May 15 for group rate. Share session dates, costs, financial aid options, and packing information. Ensure all attending adults have current YPT.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Promote from January; roster due by May 15
- **Months**: January, February, March, April, May

# Pack Field Trip Planning

Plan and coordinate pack field trips to educational and recreational destinations (Howe Caverns, MiSci, Cooperstown Farmers Museum, Liberty Ridge Farm, etc.). Confirm group pricing, set RSVP deadlines, arrange payment collection, communicate uniform and logistics details, coordinate carpooling.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: as-needed
- **When**: As opportunities arise throughout the year
- **Months**: Every month

# Responsibilities of the Council Officers

The purpose of the Boy Scouts of America to provide boys and young adults with an effective program
designed to instill desirable qualities of character, to train youth in the responsibilities of participating citizenship, and to develop in them personal fitness.  The board officers are accountable for the program.

# President Guidelines in Leading the Organization

One unique characteristic of the Boy Scouts of America is the happy and effective relationship that has
developed and is maintained between professionals and volunteers. The ratio is significant; there is on average only one professional for every 400 volunteers. This is what characterizes the Scouting movement as being primarily volunteer.

# Youth in Wood Badge

An exciting part of a Wood Badge course is the involvement of young people during the outdoor experience.  They fulfill that responsibility by providing outdoor skill instruction and facilitating the problem-solving sessions for the Wood Badge troop. Youth involvement pays dividends for the course participants, staff members, and the youth themselves.

# Foundations of Cub Scout Day Camp

To be nationally certified as a day camp director, program director or staff adviser, individuals must complete a three-part training curriculum. This online course is the first part of this training curriculum. Individuals who plan to serve as a day camp director or day camp program director must take this course prior to attending their council-led training session and before participating in the final course curriculum held in person for a day and a half at a National Camping School.

# Wood Badge CDC

All Wood Badge Course Directors/Scoutmasters are required to attend a region/area Course Directors Conference within 24 months prior to the start of the course the will serve as the Course director. The individual who is listed as the backup Course Director/Assistant Scoutmaster on the “Request for Authorization to Conduct a National Training Course” form is also required to attend a  course directors conference within 24 months prior to the start of the approved course.

# Strategic Training Plan

The Strategic Training Plan course has been developed to provide key individuals within the district and
council with the skills necessary to be a critical resource to help improve the council training program. The
A Strategic Training Plan is designed to use to help develop a flexible and adaptable plan for individuals to
use in their home district or council.

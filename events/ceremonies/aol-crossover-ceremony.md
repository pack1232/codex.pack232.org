# Arrow of Light Crossover Ceremony

Scouts who have earned the Arrow of Light badge cross over from Cub Scouting to a Scouts BSA troop. The ceremony involves the Scoutmaster and troop youth leaders from the receiving troop. Targeting January or February ensures Scouts can attend troop campouts before summer camp.

## Timeline / Checklist

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| September | AOL den leader connects with local Scouts BSA troops early |
| December | Cubmaster and AOL den leader confirm receiving troop(s); plan ceremony |
| December | Advancement Chair orders AOL badges and certificates well in advance |
| January--February | Execute crossover ceremony at a pack meeting |

## Roles Involved

- **Cubmaster** -- leads the ceremony and coordinates with the receiving Scoutmaster
- **Den Leader (AOL den)** -- prepares Scouts and families for the transition
- **Advancement Chair** -- orders and prepares Arrow of Light awards and certificates
- **AOL Crossover Coordinator** -- assists with ceremony logistics and troop coordination

# Blue & Gold Banquet

The pack's largest annual celebration, held in February to mark the anniversary of Cub Scouting's founding. The banquet recognizes Scout accomplishments, honors leaders, and includes advancement ceremonies and entertainment.

## Timeline / Checklist

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| December | Book venue and set date |
| January | Finalize food, entertainment, program, and invitations |
| February | Execute the banquet |

## Coordinators

Becky D'Anza, Andrea Dievendorf, Lindsay Knott, Kara Giglia.

## Roles Involved

- **Blue & Gold Coordinator** -- overall event lead
- **Committee Chair** -- venue logistics and budget approval
- **Program Chair** -- coordinates with pack calendar
- **Advancement Chair** -- prepares advancement awards for presentation
- **Cubmaster** -- emcees and leads ceremonies

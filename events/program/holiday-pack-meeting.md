# Holiday Pack Meeting

Holiday-themed December pack meeting. May include skits, songs, advancement ceremonies, and holiday celebration activities. This meeting closes out the fall portion of the Scouting year on a festive note.

## Timeline / Checklist

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| November | Cubmaster plans holiday theme and program |
| November | Den leaders prepare any skits or songs |
| December | Advancement Chair prepares fall awards for presentation |
| Day of meeting | Execute holiday-themed pack meeting |

## Roles Involved

- **Cubmaster** -- plans and leads the holiday program
- **Den Leaders** -- prepare den presentations, skits, or songs
- **Advancement Chair** -- presents advancement awards

# National Night Out

Annual community event hosted by Bethlehem Police Department promoting police-community partnerships, crime prevention, and neighborhood unity. Pack 232 sets up a recruitment booth alongside other community organizations.

## Schedule

5:00pm–8:00pm (setup at 4:00pm).

## Contact

Detective Sergeant Caitlyn Krage, Bethlehem PD Community Services Unit (518-603-3990).

## What to Bring

- Table, tent/canopy, chairs
- Recruitment materials, pack banner
- No electricity available on-site

## Notes

Rain or shine event. Good opportunity for visibility and fall recruitment outreach.

## Roles Involved

- **Membership Chair** -- coordinates booth setup and recruitment materials
- **Cubmaster** -- supports recruitment outreach and represents the pack

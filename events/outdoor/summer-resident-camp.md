# Summer Resident Camp

Optional annual 4-day/3-night overnight camp at Rotary Scout Reservation with Twin Rivers Council. Pack camps as a unit.

## Cost (Typical)

| Registration | Price |
|---|---|
| Cub Scout (early bird by June 1) | $275 |
| Cub Scout (after June 1) | $325 |
| Adult | $100 |
| Den Chief | $100 |

## Program

Aquatics, shooting sports, crafts, nature hikes, STEM activities, sports, first aid, scout skills, fishing, bouldering, campfire programs.

## Requirements

- All scouts MUST attend with a parent/guardian (one parent can cover multiple scouts)
- Scout must be registered Pack 232 member for group rate
- Contact Cubmaster to be added to roster before May 15
- BSA Youth Protection Training required for all adults
- BSA medical forms required

## Logistics

- Meals provided by dining hall
- Electricity available for CPAP/devices
- Pack camps using platform tents with cots
- Food allergies accommodated
- Trading post available for snacks/souvenirs

## Financial Aid

Twin Rivers Council offers camperships for families needing assistance.

## Venue & Logistics

**Rotary Scout Reservation, 279 Davitt Lake Road, Averill Park, NY 12018**

## Roles Involved

- **Cubmaster** -- manages roster and coordinates pack attendance
- **Program Chair** -- assists with program planning and logistics
- **Den Leader** -- supervises den scouts during camp

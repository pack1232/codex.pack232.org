# Summer Day Camp

Optional annual week-long day camp program run by Twin Rivers Council. Scouts attend daily with transportation available.

## Cost (Typical)

| Registration | Price |
|---|---|
| Youth | $250 |
| Youth with Parent Volunteer | $175 |
| Additional Youth | $175 |
| Optional bus transportation | ~$66–75 |

Bus pickup locations include Delmar Hannaford and Colonie Center.

## Recent Themes

- **2024:** "Wild Wild West"

## Registration

Families register individually through Twin Rivers Council. Pack coordinates group attendance.

## Venue & Logistics

**Rotary Scout Reservation, 279 Davitt Lake Road, Averill Park, NY 12018**

## Roles Involved

- **Cubmaster** -- coordinates pack group attendance
- **Program Chair** -- assists with communication and logistics

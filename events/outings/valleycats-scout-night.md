# ValleyCats Scout Night

Group outing to a Tri-City ValleyCats baseball game. Reserve group tickets in advance and coordinate attendance with pack families.

## Timeline / Checklist

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| Spring/Summer | Check ValleyCats schedule for Scout Night date |
| 2 months before | Reserve group tickets |
| 1 month before | Announce to pack families and collect RSVPs |
| Day of event | Attend the game as a pack |

## Venue & Logistics

**Joseph L. Bruno Stadium**. Group ticket reservations are typically available through the ValleyCats website or group sales office.

Jara Traina.

- **Program Chair** -- coordinates ticket reservations and logistics

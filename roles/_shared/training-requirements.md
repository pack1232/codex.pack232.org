# Training Requirements by Position

All Pack 232 leaders must complete training before working with youth. This file summarizes required and recommended trainings for each position.

---

## Universal Requirement: Youth Protection Training (YPT)

**Every registered adult** must complete Youth Protection Training before their application is approved.

- Complete at: [my.Scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org/)
- Must be renewed every **2 years**
- Print the certificate of completion and attach it to your adult application
- If already registered and current (within 2 years), you do not need to retake it

---

## Position-Specific Training Matrix

| Position | Required | Recommended | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cubmaster | YPT + Cubmaster Position-Specific Training (online at my.Scouting.org) | BALOO | Attend monthly Roundtable |
| Assistant Cubmaster | YPT + Cubmaster Position-Specific Training | BALOO | Same training as Cubmaster |
| Committee Chair | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | Online at my.Scouting.org |
| Charter Org Rep | YPT + Charter Org Rep Training + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Treasurer | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Secretary | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Den Leader | YPT + Den Leader Position-Specific Training (CS19) | BALOO | Rank-specific training available |
| Assistant Den Leader | YPT + Den Leader Position-Specific Training | — | Must be 21+ if pack's only asst. |
| Advancement Chair | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Program Chair | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | BALOO | BALOO required before any pack campout |
| Membership Chair | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Fundraising Chair | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge | — | |
| Popcorn Coordinator | YPT + Pack Committee Challenge + Popcorn Kernel Training (if applicable) | — | Council provides Popcorn training |
| Den Chief | Den Chief Training | — | Youth position; training done with den leader if possible |

**Note:** Coordinator positions under each chair (e.g., Recruitment Coordinator, Pinewood Derby Coordinator) follow the same training requirements as their parent chair position — YPT + Pack Committee Challenge at minimum.

---

## BALOO Training

Basic Adult Leader Outdoor Orientation (BALOO) is **required before any pack overnight campout** — at least one adult present must have completed BALOO.

- BALOO is highly recommended for Cubmaster, Assistant Cubmaster, and Program Chair
- See: Twin Rivers Council training calendar for upcoming BALOO offerings

---

## Trained Patch

Once position-specific training is complete (YPT + position-specific modules), a leader is considered **trained** and eligible to wear the Trained patch below their position patch.

# Den Leader Guide: Webelos (4th Grade)

## Overview

Webelos is the Cub Scouting program for fourth graders. "Webelos" stands for "We'll Be Loyal Scouts" — these Scouts are beginning the transition toward Scouts BSA. Webelos dens take on more complex adventures, begin working toward outdoor skills, and start forming connections with local Scouts BSA troops. Webelos is also when the pack's first overnight camping becomes common.

## Distinctive Features of Webelos

- **Adventure pins**: Webelos earn Adventure Pins displayed on Adventure Colors (colored ribbons worn on uniform)
- **Joint activities with troops**: Plan and conduct joint activities with a local Scouts BSA troop — gives Scouts a preview of Scouts BSA
- **Overnight camping**: Webelos dens can and should go on overnight campouts; at least one adult must have BALOO training
- **BALOO requirement**: At least one adult on any Webelos overnight must be BALOO trained

---

## Meeting Structure

1. **Before the Meeting** — Den leader, assistant, and den chief make final preparations
2. **Gathering** — Scouts join a game or activity; attendance and requirement check
3. **Opening** — Scout Oath, Scout Law, Pledge of Allegiance
4. **Activities** — Adventures focused on outdoor skills, STEM, citizenship, fitness, and other Webelos themes
5. **Closing** — Reminders, closing thought, brief ceremony; denner leads cleanup
6. **After the Meeting** — Den leader, assistant, and den chief discuss next meeting

## Webelos Uniform

- Navy blue Cub Scout uniform shirt
- Navy blue pants, shorts, or skort
- Blue web belt
- Webelos neckerchief and Webelos slide
- Navy and gold socks
- Webelos cap
- Adventure Colors (worn to display Adventure pins)

---

## Troop Relationships

As Webelos den leader:
- Identify 1–2 local Scouts BSA troops your Scouts might join next year
- Attend a troop meeting or camp with your Scouts
- Invite the Scoutmaster to speak at a den meeting
- Keep families informed about the Arrow of Light program they'll enter in 5th grade

# Pack Meeting Activity Coordinator

## Role Summary

The Pack Meeting Activity Coordinator plans the program activities for monthly pack meetings — the games, crafts, demonstrations, and interactive elements that make pack meetings fun and engaging for Scouts of all ages.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Program Chair |
| Works with | Cubmaster, Den Leaders, Snackmaster |

## Core Responsibilities

- Plan an age-appropriate activity or game for each monthly pack meeting
- Coordinate with the Cubmaster on the pack meeting agenda to fit the activity into the schedule
- Procure supplies and materials needed for activities
- Set up and facilitate the activity at the meeting; recruit parent volunteers to help
- Vary activities across the year: games, crafts, STEM activities, outdoor skills demonstrations
- Consider advancement tie-ins where possible (activities that help Scouts earn belt loops or electives)
- Clean up activity area after the meeting

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| Monthly | Plan and execute activity for each pack meeting |
| Before each meeting | Confirm activity plan with Cubmaster; procure supplies |
| September | Plan activity calendar for the year (coordinate with Cubmaster and Program Chair) |

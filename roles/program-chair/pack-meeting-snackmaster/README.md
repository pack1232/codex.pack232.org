# Pack Meeting Snackmaster

## Role Summary

The Pack Meeting Snackmaster coordinates snacks and refreshments at pack meetings, ensuring Scouts and families have something to eat and drink during or after the meeting. A simple but important role that makes pack meetings more welcoming.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Program Chair |
| Works with | Pack Meeting Activity Coordinator, Cubmaster |

## Core Responsibilities

- Plan and provide snacks/refreshments for each monthly pack meeting
- Coordinate a snack sign-up rotation among pack families (if using a shared model)
- Consider dietary restrictions and allergies — check with families at the start of the year
- Purchase or coordinate donations of snacks, drinks, plates, napkins, cups
- Set up the snack area before the meeting and clean up afterward
- Coordinate with the Treasurer on snack budget (or manage as a volunteer-donated item)
- For special events (Blue & Gold, Pinewood Derby), coordinate with the event coordinator on food

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| September | Set up snack rotation for the year; gather allergy info from families |
| Monthly | Coordinate snacks for each pack meeting |
| Special events | Coordinate with event coordinators on food needs |

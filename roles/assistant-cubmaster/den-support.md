# Assistant Cubmaster — Den Support

## Role Summary

The Den Support Assistant Cubmaster mentors den leaders, provides guidance on den meeting planning, and fills in as a substitute den leader when a den leader is absent. This role ensures den-level program quality and leader confidence.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Cubmaster |
| Works with | Den Leaders, Assistant Den Leaders, Den Chiefs |

## Core Responsibilities

- Serve as a mentor and resource for den leaders, especially new den leaders
- Fill in as substitute den leader when a den leader is unavailable
- Help den leaders plan den meetings and navigate the Cub Scout program
- Observe den meetings periodically and offer supportive feedback
- Help den leaders troubleshoot challenges (behavioral issues, low attendance, program concerns)
- Support den leader transitions when a den leader steps down or a new one starts
- Attend den leader planning meetings and share best practices across dens

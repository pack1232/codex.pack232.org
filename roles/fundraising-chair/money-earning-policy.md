# Fundraising Chair: Money-Earning Policy

## BSA Policy Overview

All unit fundraising must comply with BSA's Fiscal Policies and Procedures for Units. The key requirements:

1. **Pre-approval required** for all fundraising activities other than the annual popcorn sale
2. **Charter organization must approve** all money-earning activities
3. **Twin Rivers Council must approve** via the Unit Money-Earning Application
4. **No activities may begin** until both approvals are in hand

## The Annual Popcorn Sale

The annual Trails End popcorn sale is the only pre-approved fundraiser — no Money-Earning Application is needed. The Popcorn Coordinator manages this sale. See [`../popcorn-coordinator/README.md`](../popcorn-coordinator/README.md).

## Unit Money-Earning Application Process

For any other fundraising activity:

1. **Draft the application**: Describe the proposed fundraising activity, projected proceeds, and how funds will be used
2. **Charter org approval**: Submit to the Committee Chair, who coordinates with the Charter Org Rep (Glenmont Elementary PTA)
3. **Council approval**: Submit to Twin Rivers Council — trcscouting.org
4. **Begin only after approval**: Do not collect money or publicize the fundraiser until both approvals are received

**Application form**: `docs/Training/Positions/Treasurer/Additional Treasurer Resources/BSA/2024-11-24 - BSA Unit Money-Earning Application.md`

## Prohibited Activities

- Soliciting gifts or donations (not tax-deductible at unit level; units should not ask for donations)
- Fundraising activities not approved by council (e.g., selling items at school without approval)
- Using Scouts to solicit commercial sponsorships without approval

## Allowable Fundraising Activities (with approval)

Examples of commonly approved unit money-earning activities:
- Caroling with donation box (for a designated charity — coordinate with Community Service Coordinator)
- Car washes
- Bake sales
- Concession booth at community events

All require the Money-Earning Application.

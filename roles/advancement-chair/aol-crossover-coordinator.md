# AOL Crossover Coordinator

## Role Summary

The AOL Crossover Coordinator plans the Arrow of Light crossover ceremony — the milestone event where 5th-grade Scouts bridge from Cub Scouts to Scouts BSA. This is one of the most meaningful ceremonies in Scouting and deserves careful planning.

## Reports To

| Relationship | Role |
|---|---|
| Reports to | Advancement Chair |
| Works with | AOL Den Leader, Cubmaster, Scouts BSA Troop Scoutmaster |

## Core Responsibilities

- Plan and coordinate the Arrow of Light crossover ceremony (typically at Blue & Gold or a separate event)
- Coordinate with the AOL den leader on which Scouts are crossing over
- Coordinate with local Scouts BSA troop Scoutmaster(s) to welcome crossing-over Scouts
- Plan ceremony elements: bridge (physical or symbolic), Arrow of Light awards, troop welcome
- Ensure Arrow of Light rank requirements are completed and awards are purchased
- Communicate ceremony details to AOL families and troop leadership
- Coordinate with the Cubmaster on ceremony scripting and logistics
- Invite families and pack leadership to witness the ceremony

## Key Dates / Timeline

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| November–December | Confirm which Scouts are on track for Arrow of Light |
| January | Coordinate with troop Scoutmaster(s); begin ceremony planning |
| February–March | Execute crossover ceremony (often at Blue & Gold or a dedicated event) |
| May | Alternative crossover date if not done at Blue & Gold |

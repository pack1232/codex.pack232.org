## Broadview Credit Union (Unit Deposit Account)

The Broadview Credit Union account is the pack's primary bank account. Key points:

- All major disbursements are made by check from this account
- Account requires two authorized signatories: any two of (Cubmaster, Committee Chair, Secretary, Treasurer)
- The Committee Chair approves bills before payment
- Monthly reconciliation is done in QuickBooks Online
- Full guide: `docs/.../Pack 232 Treasurer Guide to Unit Deposit Account (UDA).md`

---

## PayPal

PayPal is used for some online payment collection and vendor payments. Key considerations:

- 2022 IRS rule: PayPal (and Venmo) must report transactions exceeding $600/year to the IRS
- Per BSA guidance (2022 memo), keep Scout account transactions separate from general pack funds
- Full guide: `docs/.../Pack 232 Treasurer Guide to PayPal.md`
- BSA memo: `docs/.../2022-03-23 - Paypal and Venmo Reporting Update.md`

---

## New York Sales Tax Exemption

Pack 232 is exempt from New York State sales tax on purchases made for the pack. The Treasurer maintains the tax-exempt certificate and ensures leaders use it for pack purchases. Contact the Treasurer before making any significant purchase to get the exemption certificate.

## Monthly Reconciliation

Each month before the committee meeting:
1. Download statements from Broadview, PayPal, and other accounts
2. Reconcile all transactions in QuickBooks Online
3. Prepare the monthly financial report for the committee

Guide: `docs/.../Pack 232 Treasurer Guide to Quickbooks Account Reconciliation.md`

# Service Chair

## Role Summary

The Service Chair helps Pack 232 give back to the community through organized service projects. Cub Scouting's commitment to "Good Turns" — acts of service for others — is a core element of the program. The Service Chair identifies service opportunities, coordinates pack participation, and helps den leaders incorporate service into their programs.

---

## Requirements

- **Age**: 21 years or older
- **Registration**: Registered adult leader of BSA; current with YPT
- **Selection**: Selected by the Committee Chair
- **Required Training**:
  - Youth Protection Training (YPT) — renew every 2 years
  - Pack Committee Challenge (online at my.Scouting.org)
- **Access**: No bank account access

---

## Core Responsibilities

- Identify and promote service projects in the charter organization (Glenmont Elementary PTA), school, and community
- Coordinate pack participation in service events (e.g., Scouting for Food)
- Help den leaders incorporate service into den meeting programs
- Suggest service project ideas to the Committee Chair and Cubmaster
- Ensure service hours are recorded (Committee Chair submits service hours to Council)
- Promote family participation in service activities
- Coordinate with Charter Org Rep on service opportunities that benefit Glenmont Elementary or PTA

---

## Service at Pack 232: Recurring Activities

| Event | Description | Coordinator |
|---|---|---|
| Scouting for Food | Annual food drive (canned goods collected door-to-door) | Kara Giglia; Service Chair supports |
| Caroling | Scouts carol in the neighborhood; proceeds to a selected beneficiary | Hank Tripp; Service Chair supports |

The Service Chair may identify additional service opportunities throughout the year.

---

## Reporting Service Hours

The Committee Chair submits service hours to Twin Rivers Council on behalf of the pack. The Service Chair should track:
- Date of service project
- Number of participants (Scouts and adults)
- Total hours served
- Description of the project

Pass this information to the Committee Chair after each service event.

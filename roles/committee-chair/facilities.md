# Committee Chair: Facilities

## Overview

Pack 232 holds its monthly pack meetings and stores some pack property at Glenmont Elementary School, which is managed through our charter organization, Glenmont Elementary PTA. The Committee Chair is responsible for securing meeting facilities and coordinating with the school.

---

## Glenmont Elementary Space Requests

To use school facilities (cafeteria, gym), the Committee Chair must submit a space request form to Glenmont Elementary. This should be done:

- At the start of each Scouting year (September) for recurring monthly pack meetings
- In advance for any special events (Pinewood Derby, Blue & Gold, etc.)

**Key spaces used by the pack:**
- **Cafeteria** — Monthly pack meetings (primary space)
- **Gym** — Pinewood Derby, larger events

**Coordinating with PTA**: As the pack's charter organization is the PTA, the Committee Chair should maintain a positive relationship with PTA leadership to ensure continued facility access and charter renewal support.

---

## Pack Property Storage

Pack property (track for Pinewood Derby, camping gear, etc.) is stored as needed. The Committee Chair, with the Cubmaster, arranges for year-round storage of the pack's Pinewood Derby track and other large items.

## Other Facilities

For events held off-site (camping, hikes, ValleyCats game, etc.), the relevant activity coordinator (Activities Chair, Popcorn Kernel, etc.) is responsible for reserving sites. The Committee Chair should be aware of and approve all facility reservations.

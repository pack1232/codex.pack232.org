# BSA Health Forms Collection

Secretary collects BSA Annual Health and Medical Record (Parts A & B) for all Scouts and registered adults. Send reminders to families who have not submitted. Health forms expire annually.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Collect at or before the September pack meeting
- **Months**: September

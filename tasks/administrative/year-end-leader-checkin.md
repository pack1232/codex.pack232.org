# Year-End Den Leader Check-In

Cubmaster conducts informal check-ins with each den leader: how did the year go, are they returning, any concerns for next year? Flags retention risks for Committee Chair.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Informal — before leaders go on summer break
- **Months**: June

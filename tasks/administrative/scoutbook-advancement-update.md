# Scoutbook Advancement Updates

Den leaders mark completed adventure requirements in Scoutbook promptly so the Advancement Chair can order awards and parents can see their Scout's progress.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: monthly
- **When**: Within 1–2 days of each den meeting
- **Months**: Every month

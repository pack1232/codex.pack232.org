# Arrow of Light Crossover: Troop Coordination

Cubmaster and AOL den leader confirm which Scouts BSA troop(s) AOL Scouts will join. Plan crossover ceremony with Scoutmaster and troop youth leaders. Target January or February so Scouts can attend troop campouts before summer camp.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: December planning for January/February crossover
- **Months**: December

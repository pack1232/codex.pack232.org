# AOL: Arrow of Light Badges & Certificates

Order Arrow of Light rank badges and certificates for all Scouts earning the award. Coordinate with AOL den leader on final Scout count. Order from Scout Shop with sufficient lead time to ensure arrival before the January/February crossover ceremony.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Order by November/December to ensure delivery for Jan/Feb crossover
- **Months**: November, December

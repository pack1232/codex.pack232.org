# Pinewood Derby Race

Annual Pinewood Derby race. Team sets up track and timing system, manages car check-in, runs the race, empanels judges for Best in Show, and distributes awards.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: March — at Glenmont Elementary gym

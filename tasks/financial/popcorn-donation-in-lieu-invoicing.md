# Popcorn Donation-in-Lieu Invoicing

Treasurer invoices Scout families who did not meet the popcorn target for "donation in lieu" — additional dues owed to make up the fundraising shortfall.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: After Scout account credits are calculated
- **Months**: November

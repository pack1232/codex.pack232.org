# Draft Upcoming-Year Budget

Treasurer drafts the pack's budget for the coming Scouting year based on prior-year actuals, planned activities, expected enrollment, and popcorn revenue projections.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Draft in July, finalize in August, present for approval in September
- **Months**: July, August

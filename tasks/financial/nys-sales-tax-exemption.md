# NY Sales Tax Exemption on Pack Purchases

Pack 232 is exempt from New York State sales tax on pack purchases. Treasurer provides the tax-exempt certificate to leaders before significant purchases. Keep receipt showing tax was not charged.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: as-needed
- **When**: Any time a significant pack purchase is made
- **Months**: Every month

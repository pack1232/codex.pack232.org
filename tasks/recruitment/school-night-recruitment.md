# School-Night Recruitment Event

Primary annual Scout recruitment event held at Glenmont Elementary. Cubmaster presents the Cub Scout program; families sign up on the spot. Follow up within 48 hours.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: Early-to-mid September, timed to school start
- **Months**: September

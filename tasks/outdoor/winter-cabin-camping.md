# Winter Cabin Camping

Annual pack winter overnight camping trip at Rotary Scout Reservation heated cabins. Reserve cabins, coordinate meals, collect medical forms and payment ($10/person). Arrange activities: sledding, hiking, campfire, games.

## Schedule

- **Frequency**: annual
- **When**: February or March — at Rotary Scout Reservation
- **Months**: February, March
